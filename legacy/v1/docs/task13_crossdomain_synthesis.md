## Section 3 — Career Ladder Destruction Signal Register

Career Ladder Destruction (CLD) signals are defined as P-position (0–5 year) Ac, V, or S calls — tasks where automation eliminates or substantially compresses a foundational learning pathway at the entry-level career stage. CLD severity is rated on a three-tier gradient:
- **High:** Primary learning mechanism eliminated; compression removes the task through which entry-level practitioners build foundational professional judgment
- **Moderate:** Meaningful reduction in developmental value; execution compressed but interpretive judgment pathway partially survives in review function
- **Low:** Administrative overhead with limited career consequence; compression is primarily an efficiency gain

### 4.3 The FC/DC Structural Pattern (Foundational Instrument Finding)

Confirmed without exception across all 6 domains and 645 tasks: H calls arise exclusively from FC Primary (Fieldcraft Primary) or SC Primary (Statecraft Primary) competency contexts. DC Primary (Datacraft Primary) competencies produce zero H calls except under explicit structural anomaly conditions (FC-secondary craft character enabling the LSD mechanism, as at 2.1.3 T3). This is a structural property of the taxonomy's craft architecture, not a coincidence of domain distribution.

Corollary: H calls in relational-institutional domains (D3, D5, D6) arise from SC Primary contexts where the practitioner's physical or relational presence is the irreducible professional contribution. No analytical complexity alone — regardless of intellectual difficulty — generates an H call.

## Section 6 — Cross-Domain Structural Findings

### Finding 1 — The Interpretive Protection Floor (Domain 2)
DC Primary competencies consistently classify A rather than S or V because the practitioner's contextual interpretation judgment — is this output valid for this watershed, this regulatory context, this management question? — is not automatable at modal organizational deployment. This protection floor operates at different levels of analytical abstraction across domains: ecological interpretation in D1, methodological design in D2 SA 2.4, governance judgment in D2 SA 2.5, and field execution reading in D2 SA 2.3. The floor is real but is eroding at the S-trajectory frontier (Section 5.2).

### Finding 2 — Paired Developmental Pathway Destruction (Domain 2)
The High-severity CLD signal at 2.2.4 T1 (computational literacy formation compressed by AI coding assistants) and the Moderate signal at 2.4.5 T1 (temporal data literacy formation compressed by AI analytical tools) are complementary disruptions to the analytical practitioner's developmental formation. Together they describe a converging threat: the review-and-certify function that protects Ac from becoming S is being constructed on a progressively hollowed developmental foundation. Workforce planning implication: environmental data science education will need structural curriculum intervention — deliberate constraint (writing code and building visualizations without AI assistance as pedagogical requirement) — as the professional workflow becomes AI-mediated.

### Finding 3 — CLD Concentration in Communication Domain
Domain 5 produces the highest absolute CLD count (15 signals) concentrated in the communication production skill areas (SA 5.1, 5.4, 5.5). This is analytically distinct from the High-severity signals in D2 (computational literacy) and D6 (Substitution). In D5, what is being compressed is foundational professional voice formation — the iterative practice through which entry-level practitioners learn to read audiences, select emphasis, and develop narrative judgment. AI content generation tools accelerate production at exactly the developmental stage where inefficiency served a learning function. The D5 communication production CLD profile is the most dispersed in the taxonomy (9 Moderate signals spread across content types) and may be the most difficult to address through curriculum intervention because the compressed tasks are embedded in daily workflow rather than discrete training activities.

### Finding 4 — The Relational Core is Automation-Resistant
87 of 136 H calls (64%) arise from relational-institutional legitimacy mechanisms, concentrated in D3, D5, and D6. These competencies — facilitation, negotiation, tribal consultation, community organizing, board governance, conflict resolution, accountability management — show no trajectory toward S or V in any domain. The relational core of environmental management is not merely difficult to automate; it is constitutively practitioner-dependent. An AI system cannot bear institutional accountability for a government-to-government consultation outcome, cannot read the real-time dynamics of a contentious public meeting, and cannot serve as the legitimate embodiment of professional authority in an adaptive management decision. These are structural protections, not temporary technological limitations.

### Finding 5 — V-Cluster Geography
V calls cluster geographically in two zone types: (a) continuously-operating automated platform zones (data monitoring infrastructure, acoustic species identification pipelines, automated QC systems, ESG reporting platforms, financial management systems), and (b) physical automation supervision zones (drone autonomous mission execution, GPS-guided equipment operation, sensor network data collection). In both zone types, V calls are surrounded by practitioner-primary A calls — the V execution layer is protected above by scoping functions and below by interpretation functions that remain practitioner-primary. No V call has expanded to adjacent tasks across any domain.

### Finding 6 — FC/DC Structural Pattern Confirmed at Scale
The FC/DC structural pattern — H calls arise from FC Primary or SC Primary competency contexts; DC Primary competencies produce zero H calls except under explicit structural anomaly conditions — holds without exception across all 645 tasks in all 6 domains. This is the most robust structural finding in the taxonomy and should anchor the Delphi instrument framing. It means H calls are not primarily a function of cognitive complexity or professional difficulty but of the craft character of the competency — the irreducibility of embodied presence (FC) or relational-institutional authority (SC) to the professional contribution.

### Finding 7 — Domain 2 as Taxonomic Inflection Point
Domain 2 (Technical Skills & Data Management) is the domain where the taxonomy's major structural tensions converge. It has: the highest Ac rate (37%), the only S-trajectory flags for data processing tasks, the only D-series CLD signals for computational and analytical literacy formation, the only structural anomaly H call, and the first V calls outside governance/monitoring contexts. Domain 2 represents the profession's primary exposure surface to AI disruption — not because the domain's tasks are easily substituted, but because the developmental pathways through which practitioners build the judgment capacity to resist substitution are exactly what AI tools are compressing.

## Section 7 — Instrument Standing Rules (Cumulative)

The following standing rules govern any future application of the exposure classification instrument, derived from D1 through D6 classification work.

**R1 — FC/DC Structural Rule:** Any H call on a DC Primary competency is anomalous and requires explicit LSD or ARM mechanism documentation. FC Primary → watch for H; DC Primary → A expected.

**R2 — T3 Horizon Pattern:** T3 tasks with synthesis/recommendation/planning function → Horizon 2. T3 tasks with monitoring/assessment/verification function → Horizon 1. Rule is functional, not positional.

**R3 — Technology-Integrated FC Rule:** Where technology is present as a universal standard tool (not a mode-split), classify A with field-H character documented. "Technology as standard tool" → A; "technology vs. no-technology as organizational mode-split" → modal A or H with mode-split flag.

**R4 — A/V Modal Deployment Rule:** Classify based on current deployment reality at modal organizational deployment. The automated system as continuous primary executor → V. Practitioner as primary executor with automated tools → A or Ac. Modal deployment, not frontier deployment, determines classification; mode-split flag documents where modal and frontier diverge.

**R5 — S-Confirmation Checklist (D5-2):** S confirmed only when all three criteria resolve in favor of substitution:
- Criterion 1 — Full task performance: Can automated system execute complete task without meaningful human contribution? "Meaningful" = judgment that would change outputs, not artifact management.
- Criterion 2 — Scoping/interpretation bracket: Is task bracketed by practitioner-primary T1 (scoping) and T3 (interpretation)? If yes, apply D3-3 protocol (Ac with S-trajectory flag).
- Criterion 3 — Modal deployment: Does S condition hold at modal organizational deployment or only at frontier?

**R6 — V-Cluster in SC/DC Domains (D3-1):** V classification is possible in SC Primary domains when DC Primary sub-competencies deploy continuously-operating automated platforms at organizational scale. V-cluster geography: automated platform zones and physical automation supervision zones. V calls do not expand to adjacent tasks where scoping and interpretation remain practitioner-primary.

**R7 — Dual Ac Pattern (D3-2):** Track P-position Ac (career ladder destruction signals) and C-position Ac (analytical execution compression) separately. Both are Ac but carry fundamentally different workforce planning implications. Apply CLD severity gradient to P-position Ac only.

**R8 — Established-Methodology Compression Protocol (D3-3):** DC Primary T2 execution tasks using standardized methodology and mature databases classify Ac (not S) when T1 scoping and T3 interpretation remain practitioner-primary. Flag as S-trajectory when: (i) methodology is highly standardized with controlled vocabulary, AND (ii) underlying database is mature and broadly applicable. Refer to Delphi.

**R9 — CLD Severity Gradient (D5-3):** High = primary learning mechanism eliminated; Moderate = meaningful reduction in developmental value, review function partially survives; Low = administrative overhead, minimal career consequence.

**R10 — V-Trajectory Flag (D5-4):** Apply where V threshold not yet crossed at modal deployment but anticipated within 3–5 year horizon as platform adoption increases. Preserves accurate current-state classification while documenting anticipated displacement.

**R11 — Cv Boundary (Domain 4):** Convergent (Cv) classification requires AI and physical robotics to be operationally inseparable during task execution at modal professional deployment. AI-assisted augmentation of practitioner-operated equipment → AI vector only. Semi-autonomous physical platform with AI navigation layer as standard operational mode → Cv.

**R12 — Source-Text Register Effect:** Source text active-verb framing (analyze, interpret, design) maps to A under the instrument criterion. If practitioners in expert review would describe their relationship to an automated system as supervisory rather than as primary executors, V overrides source-text active framing. Source text does not determine classification; deployment reality does.
