## Section 1 — Domain Character Summary

Domain 3 (Management & Administration) presents the highest Compressive Augmentation (Ac) rate in the taxonomy and the highest Human-Essential (H) count per skill area, producing a distribution that at first appears paradoxical: the domain with the most administrative overhead — the most compressible work — also produces the most relational-institutional legitimacy H calls. The paradox resolves cleanly once the Statecraft architecture of the domain is understood. What is compressible in Domain 3 is administrative execution: budget tracking, expense documentation, records maintenance, ESG data collection, HR documentation, asset inventorying, report compilation, and established-methodology computation. What is not compressible is the governance fabric that gives those administrative acts their institutional consequence: contract negotiation, board presentation, supervisory accountability relationships, adaptive management facilitation, change management leadership, investor engagement, and circular economy partnership building. AI can process the documentation; it cannot exercise the authority that makes the documentation matter.

Across 105 tasks covering 35 competencies in 7 skill areas, Domain 3 produces: 59 Augmentation (A, 56%), 24 Compressive Augmentation (Ac, 23%), 16 Human-Essential (H, 15%), 5 Supervision (V, 5%), and 0 Substitution or Robotics/Convergent calls. The vector distribution is uniformly AI across all non-H tasks. Zero Ro or Cv exposure — Domain 3 is pure knowledge-governance work with no physical execution layer.

The Ac rate of 23% is the highest in the taxonomy and confirms the domain hypothesis: administrative, documentation, compliance tracking, reporting, and established-methodology computation tasks are the primary AI compression targets in management work. The 10 confirmed P-position CLD signals concentrate in administrative learning tasks that have historically been the entry pathway to management competency — budget preparation, expense tracking, Gantt chart construction, ESG data collection, HR documentation, asset tracking, records maintenance — all facing compression to review-and-certify functions.

The H calls are Domain 3's structural signature: 16 total, concentrated in 3.2 (5 calls) and 3.5 (7 calls), with the mechanism uniformly relational-institutional legitimacy. Every H call falls on a task where either the practitioner's ongoing relational presence is the professional contribution (supervisory relationships, coaching, culture building, change management), an accountability relationship determines whether the outcome is institutionally legitimate (board presentations, investor engagements, disclosure governance), or live social dynamics cannot be mediated through technology without losing the essential quality of the engagement (facilitation, cross-team coordination, cross-generational knowledge exchange). Zero H calls appear on analytical or planning tasks regardless of their intellectual complexity — the H mechanism in Domain 3 is not cognitive demand but relational-institutional authority.

The V-cluster (5 calls, all in 3.4 and 3.6 DC Primary sub-competencies) is Domain 3's most structurally novel finding: continuously-operating automated financial and ESG platforms at organizational scale shift the practitioner role from primary executor to supervisor at modal deployment. This extends beyond Domain 4's physical robotics V pattern to a purely knowledge-governance context, establishing that V is possible in Statecraft-dominant domains when DC Primary automated systems are the continuous operational actor.

## Section 5 — New Instrument Findings

The following findings arose during Domain 3 classification and must be carried forward to Domains 5 and 2. Standing rules established in prior domains continue to apply without re-statement.

**Finding D3-1 — V-Cluster in Statecraft-Dominant Domains**
V classification is possible in SC Primary domains when DC Primary sub-competencies deploy continuously-operating automated platforms at organizational scale. The practitioner role shifts from A (primary executor with tool) to V (supervisor of automated outputs) when: (a) the automated system is the continuous primary operator of the task at modal organizational deployment, and (b) the practitioner's role is genuinely supervisory — reviewing outputs, interpreting anomalies, authorizing exceptions — rather than primary execution with AI assistance. In Domain 3, this pattern concentrated in 3.4 (financial management platforms) and 3.6 (ESG data platforms), all on DC Primary competencies embedded within the SC-dominant domain. This extends Finding 5.4 from Domain 1 (A/V source-text framing): source text register does not determine classification; deployment reality does. For Domains 5 and 2: watch for V calls wherever automated monitoring, reporting, or processing platforms operate continuously as primary systems. Domain 2 (Technical Skills) is the highest-probability zone for additional V calls, particularly in 2.6 (AI-enhanced monitoring) where fully automated sensor networks and species identification pipelines may qualify.

**Finding D3-2 — Dual Ac Pattern: P-Position vs. C-Position Compressive Augmentation**
Domain 3 produces both P-position Ac (career ladder destruction signals, 10 confirmed) and C-position Ac (analytical execution compression, 14 confirmed). These carry fundamentally different workforce planning implications and must be tracked separately in the cross-domain synthesis. P-position Ac eliminates developmental learning pathways — it removes the tasks through which entry-level practitioners build professional judgment, producing a generation of practitioners who inherit the review-and-certify function without having executed the underlying work. C-position Ac compresses established expertise without necessarily blocking entry, but it does narrow the experienced practitioner's value-add to interpretive and governance functions, concentrating analytical execution in automated systems. The severity gradient established for P-position signals (High: primary learning mechanism eliminated; Moderate: meaningful reduction in developmental value; Low: administrative overhead with limited career consequence) applies within both categories. For Domains 5 and 2: Domain 5 (Communication) is expected to produce minimal Ac of either type — relational competencies do not compress the same way as administrative or analytical execution. Domain 2 is expected to produce the highest C-position Ac rate in the taxonomy, concentrated in data processing, computational modeling, and monitoring tasks.

**Finding D3-3 — Established-Methodology Compression Rule (S-Trajectory Identification Protocol)**
DC Primary competencies executing established computational methodologies (LCA per ISO 14040/14044 in 3.7.3; MFA per material accounting standards in 3.7.5) at the analytical execution task (T2 in a T1-T2-T3 sequence) classify Ac when: (a) automated platforms substantially compress execution time, and (b) the S threshold is not crossed because scoping (T1) and interpretation (T3) remain practitioner-primary. However, when both conditions below are met, flag as S-trajectory and refer to Delphi: (i) the methodology is highly standardized with a controlled vocabulary of impact categories, system boundary conventions, and calculation procedures; and (ii) the underlying database is mature and broadly applicable to the product or material category in question (e.g., ecoinvent for common consumer goods, established supply chain transparency databases). The S-trajectory designation signals not that the task is currently S, but that the scoping and interpretation functions (T1 and T3) are themselves at risk of compression as AI interpretation tools mature — at which point the full T1-T2-T3 sequence becomes S. Apply this flag to any T2 within a DC Primary T1-T2-T3 sequence where methodology is standardized and database is mature. In Domain 2, this rule is expected to apply broadly across data processing, remote sensing analysis, and AI-enhanced monitoring tasks — Domain 2 is the first domain where confirmed S calls are anticipated.
