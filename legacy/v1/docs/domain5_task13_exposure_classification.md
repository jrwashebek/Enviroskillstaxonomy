## Section 1 — Domain Character Summary

Domain 5 (Communication & Stakeholder Engagement) is the Statecraft-dominant domain at 93% SC Primary, and it produces the most structurally bifurcated automation exposure profile in the taxonomy. The domain divides cleanly into two analytically distinct halves with opposing profiles:

**The relational-institutional half** (5.2 Stakeholder Engagement, 5.3 Cultural Competency, 5.6 Social Science & Behavior Change) is Human-Essential dominant — 21 of 45 tasks (47%), the highest H density in the taxonomy, exceeding even Domain 6's governance core. The mechanism is consistently the two SC-domain H sub-mechanisms: the Live Social Dynamics Mechanism fires on facilitation, mediation, community engagement, and live interpretation tasks where real-time reading and adjustment to human group dynamics in physical or relational space is the essential quality; the Accountability Relationship Mechanism fires on government-to-government consultation, MOU negotiation, institutional representation, and community-based research accountability where who is present and accountable determines whether the outcome is institutionally legitimate.

**The production-and-service half** (5.1 Public Communication, 5.4 Education & Outreach, 5.5 Customer Service) is Compressive Augmentation and Supervision dominant — Ac = 15, V = 7 of 45 tasks (49%). AI content generation tools (LLMs, Canva AI, platform-native AI) are deployed against the full set of content production tasks, and AI analytics platforms and permit management systems cross the V threshold in monitoring and service delivery contexts.

## Section 5 — New Instrument Findings

The following findings arose during Domain 5 classification and must be carried forward to Domain 2.

**Finding D5-1 — LSD/ARM Boundary Robustness Confirmed Across SC-Dominant Domain**
The two SC-domain H sub-mechanisms (Live Social Dynamics and Accountability Relationship) fired without exception across all 35 H calls. Zero anomalous H calls appeared on non-relational tasks regardless of cognitive complexity. The mechanisms are robust and do not require re-derivation in Domain 2. For Domain 2 (Technical Skills): H calls will be rare and will require explicit LSD or ARM mechanism documentation where they occur; any H call on a DC Primary competency in Domain 2 is anomalous and requires explicit reasoning (extending D1 Finding 5.1 from FC/DC to DC-primary specific in a Technical domain context).

**Finding D5-2 — S-Boundary Threshold: Full-Interaction Criterion Preserved**
The S threshold was explicitly adjudicated at 5.5.1 T1 (visitor information services) and held at Ac + mode-split rather than S because the full-interaction criterion was not met at modal staffed deployment. The criterion as written ("AI handles the full interaction without meaningful practitioner contribution") is workable but requires a modal deployment stipulation: the classification applies to modal deployment, not to the most-automated deployment scenario available. Mode-split flags document where modal and advanced-deployment diverge. For Domain 2: confirmed S calls are anticipated (per D3-3 S-trajectory identification protocol) in data processing, AI-enhanced monitoring, and established-methodology computation tasks — these will be the first confirmed S calls outside Domain 6.

**Finding D5-3 — CLD Severity Gradient Applied to P-Position Ac**
Domain 5 produces 14 P-position CLD signals, requiring systematic severity differentiation. Applied three-tier gradient:
- **High:** Primary learning mechanism eliminated — task was the entry-level pathway through which foundational professional judgment was built (5.2.2 T3 meeting documentation; 5.5.1 T1 visitor contact; 5.5.2 T1/T3 permit processing). These represent the most consequential workforce access disruption.
- **Moderate:** Meaningful reduction in developmental value — execution compressed but interpretive judgment pathway partially survives (5.1.1 T1/T2; 5.1.2 T3; 5.1.4 T1; 5.1.5 T1/T2; 5.4.2 T1; 5.4.5 T1; 5.5.3 T3).
- **Low:** Administrative overhead with limited career consequence — compressed tasks were not the primary mechanism for developing professional judgment (5.4.1 T2; 5.4.3 T3).

**Finding D5-4 — V-Trajectory Flag as Instrument Addition**
Domain 5 introduced a new flag type at 5.3.4 T3 (DEI monitoring): V-trajectory, used where the V threshold has not been crossed at modal current deployment but is expected to cross within the 3–5 year horizon as platform adoption increases. This flag preserves accurate current-state classification (A or Ac) while documenting anticipated displacement. Recommended addition to instrument flag definitions for use in Domain 2, particularly in AI-enhanced monitoring and digital reporting contexts where platform adoption is uneven.
