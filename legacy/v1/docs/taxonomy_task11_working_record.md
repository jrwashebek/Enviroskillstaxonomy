## DOMAIN 1: Ecological & Scientific Knowledge

**Dominant craft: Datacraft (26/40, 65%). Fieldcraft (14/40, 35%). Zero Statecraft Primary.**

Domain 1 is a mixed-craft domain: the field science cohort (systematic observation, survey, measurement, and diagnosis competencies) is Fieldcraft Primary; the analytical science cohort (modeling, genetics, climate projection, and applied planning competencies) is Datacraft Primary. The 35% Fieldcraft rate is Domain 1's structural feature, not a calibration anomaly — it reflects the field science foundation on which Domain 1's analytical work rests.

Four Statecraft Secondary appearances in Domain 1 converge with Task 10's S3 Secondary and consultation-reform flags (novel ecosystem, assisted migration, TEK-adjacent competencies) — three independent frameworks identifying governance dimensions in scientific content.

## DOMAIN 3: Management & Administration

**Dominant craft: Statecraft (27/35, 77%). Datacraft (8/35, 23%). Zero Fieldcraft Primary.**

Domain 3 is a Statecraft-dominant domain with a coherent Datacraft minority. The Datacraft Primary cluster concentrates in two sub-areas: digital financial systems (3.4.1, 3.4.3, 3.4.4, 3.4.5) where the defining deliverable is technical infrastructure, and analytical methodologies embedded in management practice (3.6.5, 3.7.3, 3.7.5) where formal quantitative methods are the irreducible professional contribution.

**Paradigm illustration for Delphi packet — the budgeting split**: 3.1.2 (budget development = Statecraft) vs. 3.4.1 (digital financial systems = Datacraft). Same subject matter; different defining deliverables; different craft assignments. Lead with this when explaining craft assignment methodology to panelists.

**⊗ Rule: Established-methodology-equals-Datacraft** first applies in Domain 3: LCA (3.7.3, ISO 14040/14044) and MFA (3.7.5, material accounting methodology) receive Datacraft Primary because their professional value is irreducibly methodological, not because their domain context is Datacraft-dominant.

## DOMAIN 4: Field Operations & Implementation

**Dominant craft: Fieldcraft (35/35, 100%). Zero Datacraft Primary. Zero Statecraft Primary.**

Domain 4 is the purest Fieldcraft domain in the taxonomy. Every competency has a defining deliverable that is a place-based field output — restored site, executed burn, managed field operation, installed infrastructure — requiring embodied judgment that cannot be developed to professional standard without sustained field experience.

Statecraft Secondary appears on 12 competencies (34%) where field operations are embedded in permit conditions — specifying the minimum institutional navigation capacity field practitioners need. This is a training design finding, not just a tagging artifact.

## DOMAIN 6: Policy, Planning, Law & Economics

**Dominant craft: Statecraft (33/40, 83%). Datacraft (7/40, 18%). Zero Fieldcraft Primary. Zero Fieldcraft Secondary.**

Domain 6 is the governance architecture domain. The seven Datacraft Primary competencies form a coherent cluster defined by the ⊗ Rule: all are established professional methodologies with formal standards (EPA RAGS, TCFD, InVEST/ARIES, IMPLAN, ISO 14064, toxicokinetic methodology) whose irreducible value is the verified analytical characterization they produce.

**Prediction note**: Domain 6 Datacraft Primary was predicted at 25–30% but landed at 18%. The shortfall arose from overestimating the analytical character of market mechanism design, conservation finance instrument development, and climate finance design — all of which are governance architecture work (Statecraft Primary) rather than economic analysis.

**Complete S4 and Fieldcraft absence**: Domain 6 produces zero S4 Primary/Secondary (Task 10) and zero Fieldcraft Primary/Secondary (Task 11) — both absent for the same structural reason. The governance architecture and field operations domains are structurally inverse with no craft or workflow overlap.
