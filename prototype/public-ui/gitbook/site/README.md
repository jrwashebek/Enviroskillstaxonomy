# Find your way into EMST

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](how-to-read/drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

EMST describes environmental jobs: the tasks people do, the skills behind them, and how the work differs from one employer to another. It is a free, open draft.

This guidebook explains EMST and points you to the right records. The records themselves are in the EMST explorer.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Never for decisions about people.** EMST describes work. It is not a job analysis, a basis for classification, a crediting plan or an assessment. Do not use it to hire, to set pay or grade, to rate performance, to discipline staff or to choose who to lay off.
{% endhint %}

## Start with what you need to do

- **[Describe the work](by-need/describe-the-work.md).** Duties for a workforce plan, a team charter or the first draft of a position description.
- **[Develop people and programs](by-need/develop-people-and-programs.md).** What a course or training program could cover. Explore only, for now.
- **[Find my next move](by-need/find-my-next-move.md).** Roles that share work with yours, and what differs. Never a recommendation.
- **[Anticipate change](by-need/anticipate-change.md).** Early signals about technology, policy and the job market, and what EMST can't say yet.
- **[Use, check and improve the data](by-need/use-check-and-improve-the-data.md).** Download, cite, reuse with AI tools, and report problems.
