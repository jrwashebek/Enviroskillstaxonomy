# Glossary

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

Plain meanings of the abbreviations and specialist words you will see on EMST. Where a term is an abbreviation, the second column spells it out.

| Term | Stands for | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ADA | Americans with Disabilities Act | A US civil rights law for people with disabilities; its Title II covers state and local government services, including websites. |
| adaptive management |  | Managing in cycles: act, watch the results, learn, then adjust. |
| adverse action |  | A formal action against an employee, such as a suspension, a demotion or a removal. |
| AI | artificial intelligence | Computer systems that do tasks such as writing text, sorting data or spotting objects in images. |
| BLS | Bureau of Labor Statistics | The federal agency that publishes US data on jobs, pay and future job growth. |
| burn boss |  | The person in charge of a prescribed fire on the ground. |
| CAA | Clean Air Act | The main US law for controlling air pollution. |
| CEQ | Council on Environmental Quality | A White House office that gives the President advice on environmental policy. |
| CEQA | California Environmental Quality Act | California's law that makes state and local agencies study a project's effects on the environment before they decide on it. |
| CFM | Certified Floodplain Manager | A credential for people who manage floodplains and flood risk. |
| CFR | Code of Federal Regulations | The collection of current federal rules, arranged by subject. |
| chain of custody |  | The signed record of who handled a sample or piece of evidence, and when. |
| CHMM | Certified Hazardous Materials Manager | A credential for people who manage hazardous materials. |
| class specification |  | A state or local government's description of a job class, with its duties, minimum qualifications and pay range. |
| classification standard |  | OPM's guide for deciding a federal job's series, title and grade. |
| conservation corps |  | A program that pays people, often young adults, to do conservation work on crews while they train. |
| crediting plan |  | A guide HR uses to judge whether an applicant's experience meets a job's requirements. |
| CWA | Clean Water Act | The main US law for controlling pollution in rivers, lakes, wetlands and coastal waters. |
| data craft |  | Skill in turning field notes and measurements into evidence people can trust, including checking work done by AI. |
| Delphi panel |  | A group of experts who rate items on their own over several rounds, seeing a summary each time, until they agree. |
| DOI | US Department of the Interior | The federal department whose agencies manage national parks, wildlife refuges and other public lands. |
| DOJ | US Department of Justice | The federal department that enforces federal law and gives legal advice to agencies. |
| DOL | US Department of Labor | The federal department for workers, job training and job data; it sponsors O\*NET. |
| EA | environmental assessment | A shorter NEPA document that helps an agency decide whether it needs a full environmental impact statement. |
| ECQ | Executive Core Qualifications | OPM's list of leadership skills needed to join the Senior Executive Service. |
| EHS | environment, health and safety | The part of a business that manages pollution, worker safety and the rules for both. |
| EIS | environmental impact statement | The most detailed NEPA document, written when a federal action may have major effects on the environment. |
| EO | executive order | A written order from the President that directs federal agencies. |
| EPA | Environmental Protection Agency | The federal agency that sets and enforces national rules on pollution. |
| ESA | Endangered Species Act | The US law that protects plants and animals at risk of dying out, and the places they live. |
| ESCO | European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations | The European Union's list of skills and occupations, in many languages. |
| ESG | environmental, social and governance | Topics beyond profit that companies report on and investors look at. |
| FAA | Federal Aviation Administration | The federal agency that oversees flying in the US, including drones. |
| field craft |  | Skill in sensing, measuring and acting safely in a real place, learned through supervised time in the field. |
| FR | Federal Register | The daily federal publication of new rules, proposed rules and notices. |
| FWCI | Federal Workforce Competency Initiative | OPM's project to build competency models for federal jobs. |
| GHG | greenhouse gas | A gas, such as carbon dioxide or methane, that traps heat in the air. |
| GIS | geographic information system | Software and methods for making maps and studying where things are. |
| ground-truth |  | To check a map, model or remote sensing product against what is really on the ground. |
| GS | General Schedule | The main federal pay system for office and professional jobs, with grades from GS-1 to GS-15. |
| hazard mitigation |  | Action taken before a disaster to lower long-term risk to people and property. |
| HAZWOPER | Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response | The OSHA safety standard, and its training, for work at hazardous waste sites and chemical spills. |
| ICS | Incident Command System | A standard way to organize people and resources for emergencies and wildfires. |
| IDP | individual development plan | A written plan that an employee and a supervisor agree on to build skills. |
| IQCS | Incident Qualifications and Certification System | A federal database that tracks wildland fire training and qualifications. |
| IQR | interquartile range | The spread of the middle half of a set of ratings; a small IQR means most raters agree. |
| job analysis |  | A study an employer does, with subject-matter experts, to record a job's tasks and the knowledge and skills it needs. |
| lidar | light detection and ranging | A laser method that measures distance, used to map land and forests in three dimensions. |
| MS4 | municipal separate storm sewer system | A public system of drains and pipes that carries stormwater and needs a Clean Water Act permit. |
| NEPA | National Environmental Policy Act | The US law that makes federal agencies look at the effects of a proposed action on the environment before they decide. |
| NFIP | National Flood Insurance Program | The federal program that offers flood insurance where communities adopt floodplain rules. |
| NHPA | National Historic Preservation Act | The US law that protects historic places; its Section 106 sets out a review for federal projects. |
| NIFC | National Interagency Fire Center | The center in Boise, Idaho, where US agencies coordinate support for wildland fires. |
| NPDES | National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System | The Clean Water Act permit program for releasing pollutants into US waters. |
| NRCS | Natural Resources Conservation Service | A USDA agency that helps farmers and other private landowners protect soil, water and habitat. |
| NSR | New Source Review | Clean Air Act permits needed to build or change a large source of air pollution. |
| NWCG | National Wildfire Coordinating Group | A group of US fire agencies that sets shared standards for wildland fire training and jobs. |
| O\*NET | Occupational Information Network | The US Department of Labor's free database of the tasks, skills and tools of hundreds of occupations. |
| O\*NET-SOC | O\*NET Standard Occupational Classification codes | The job codes O\*NET uses, based on the federal SOC codes. |
| OIRA | Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs | The part of OMB that reviews major draft federal rules before they are issued. |
| OMB | Office of Management and Budget | The White House office that prepares the federal budget and oversees how agencies work. |
| OPM | Office of Personnel Management | The federal agency that sets HR rules for federal jobs, such as job series and qualification standards. |
| OPM series |  | A four-digit code OPM uses to group federal jobs by type of work, such as 0460 for forestry. |
| OSHA | Occupational Safety and Health Administration | The federal agency that sets and enforces workplace safety rules. |
| PD | position description | The document that lists a job's duties and requirements; in federal jobs it is the basis for setting series and grade. |
| PE | Professional Engineer | A state license that lets an engineer sign and seal engineering work. |
| PFAS | per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances | A large group of human-made chemicals that last a very long time, often called “forever chemicals”. |
| positive education requirement |  | A rule that federal jobs in some series need certain degrees or courses, not just experience. |
| prescribed fire |  | Fire set on purpose, under a written plan and the right weather, to meet land goals; also called a prescribed burn. |
| PSD | Prevention of Significant Deterioration | Clean Air Act permits that keep the air from getting worse in places where it is already clean enough. |
| QA | quality assurance | The planned steps that make sure work and data meet a set standard. |
| QC | quality control | The checks that find and fix errors in work or data. |
| QEP | Qualified Environmental Professional | A credential for people who work in environmental fields. |
| qualification standard |  | OPM's statement of the least education and experience that federal jobs in a series need. |
| RCRA | Resource Conservation and Recovery Act | The US law on handling solid and hazardous waste from the time it is made until it is disposed of. |
| reclamation |  | Returning land disturbed by mining or drilling to a stable and useful state. |
| REHS | Registered Environmental Health Specialist | A credential for environmental health inspectors, also called RS (Registered Sanitarian). |
| remediation |  | Cleaning up polluted soil, water or sites. |
| remote sensing |  | Gathering facts about land, water or air from a distance, using satellites, aircraft or drones. |
| RIF | reduction in force | The formal process an employer uses to cut jobs and lay off staff. |
| riparian |  | Describes the land along the banks of a river, stream or lake. |
| Section 106 |  | The part of the National Historic Preservation Act that makes federal agencies consider effects on historic places before they act. |
| Section 404 |  | The part of the Clean Water Act that requires a permit to put dredged or fill material into waters, including many wetlands. |
| Section 7 |  | The part of the Endangered Species Act under which federal agencies consult wildlife agencies so that their actions do not put listed species at risk. |
| SES | Senior Executive Service | The group of top federal career managers just below political appointees. |
| silviculture |  | The practice of growing and tending forests to meet set goals. |
| SME | subject-matter expert | A person with deep, hands-on knowledge of the work being described. |
| SOC | Standard Occupational Classification | The federal system that sorts jobs into occupations for statistics, each with a number code. |
| specialized experience |  | Experience that gave an applicant the specific skills a federal job needs, as the job announcement describes them. |
| statecraft |  | Skill in turning knowledge into decisions that hold up, by working through laws, agencies, budgets and people. |
| stop-work authority |  | The right and duty of any worker to stop a job they believe is unsafe. |
| task book |  | A record of tasks a trainee must do while an evaluator watches, used to earn a qualification, as in wildland fire. |
| TEGL | Training and Employment Guidance Letter | A guidance letter from the Department of Labor to the states and others who run job training programs. |
| Title V |  | The part of the Clean Air Act that requires operating permits for large sources of air pollution. |
| TMDL | total maximum daily load | The most of a pollutant a water body can take and still meet water quality standards, and the plan to get there. |
| UAS | uncrewed aircraft system | A drone, plus its controls and the people who fly it. |
| USDA | US Department of Agriculture | The federal department that includes the Forest Service and the Natural Resources Conservation Service. |
| USFS | US Forest Service | The USDA agency that manages national forests and grasslands. |
| WG | Wage Grade | The federal pay system for trades, crafts and labor jobs. |
| WIOA | Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act | The federal law that funds job training and local workforce boards. |
