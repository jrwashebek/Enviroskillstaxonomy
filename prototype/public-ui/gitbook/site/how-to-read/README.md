# How to read EMST

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

This section explains every label you will see on EMST. In the explorer, each label on a record links to its explanation.

- **[What you can use EMST for](uses.md).** What each use label allows, from explore only to job analysis input, and which uses no record reaches yet.
- **[Drafted by AI agents](drafted-by-ai-agents.md).** Who drafted EMST's records, what AI agents and people did, and what has not been done yet.
- **[Review status](review-status.md).** The review statuses a record can have, and where release records stand.
- **[Evidence labels: E, J and S](evidence-labels.md).** The E, J and S labels that say what each record rests on.
- **[Assessment pending](assessment-pending.md).** Why some values show “Assessment pending” instead of a number, and what that does and doesn't mean.
- **[How established the work is](how-established.md).** Labels that say how established a kind of work is, from on watch to withdrawn.
- **[Links to other systems](links-to-other-systems.md).** How EMST records link to O\*NET, ESCO, OPM job series and state job classes, and what the match types mean.
- **[Source grades (A to D)](source-grades.md).** The A to D grades given to the sources of signals about change.
- **[Formation tasks](formation-tasks.md).** Tasks that are learned through supervised practice on the job, not from a course alone.
- **[Not a government website](not-a-government-website.md).** EMST is an independent, open project. No government agency runs it or has endorsed it.
- **[Held records](held-records.md).** The statement on held records, and how many there are.
- **[Glossary](glossary.md).** Plain-language meanings of the abbreviations and specialist words used across EMST.
