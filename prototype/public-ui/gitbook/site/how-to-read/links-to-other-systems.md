# Links to other systems

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

Some records link to O\*NET, ESCO, OPM job series or state job classes. Each link has a match type.

| Match type | What it means |
|---|---|
| Close fit, not the same | Much of the meaning is shared, but not all of it. |
| Broader | The other item covers more than the EMST item. |
| More specific | The other item covers less than the EMST item. |
| Related in part | The two items overlap only in part. |
| Mirror role | Similar work, seen from the other side of a permit or an inspection. For example, a state inspector and the person at a plant who deals with the inspector. |
| Not a match | An AI agent noted that these items are different, to avoid confusion. |
| No match found | The AI agents looked and found nothing that fits. |

Every link is “AI-suggested, not reviewed”. Some links also say whether two AI coders agreed, or disagreed and need a person to decide.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Not a claim that EMST matches these systems.** AI agents suggested these links to help you find related items. No person has checked them. They do not mean EMST matches O\*NET, ESCO, OPM or any state system, and EMST makes no such claim until people review them.
{% endhint %}

We show no confidence numbers on screen. The AI agents' own numbers are not calibrated, so they would look more exact than they are. The data files keep them.
