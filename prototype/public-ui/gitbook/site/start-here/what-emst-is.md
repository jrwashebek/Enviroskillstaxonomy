# What EMST is

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](../how-to-read/drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

EMST stands for Environmental Management Skills Taxonomy. It describes environmental work. It lists the tasks people do and the skills behind them, which EMST calls competencies. It also shows how a role differs from one employer to another, such as a federal agency, a state, a nonprofit or a firm.

## What is in it

- **Roles.** 37 role profiles, such as a forester or an environmental permit writer.
- **Tasks.** 1,317 short statements of the work, each linked to the roles that do it.
- **Competencies.** 223 skills and bodies of knowledge behind the tasks.
- **Employer versions.** 140 versions of the roles, one for each kind of employer that has the role.
- **Subject modules.** 19 groups of related work, such as air quality, plus core practice that every module shares.
- **Signals of change.** 107 signs that the work may change, all on watch.
- **Links to other systems.** Links to O\*NET, ESCO, OPM job series and state job classes. AI agents suggested every link, and no person has reviewed them.

[How EMST is put together](../how-emst-works/README.md) explains how these parts link.

## What it is not

{% hint style="warning" %}
EMST describes work. It is not a job analysis, a basis for classification, a crediting plan or an assessment. Do not use it to hire, to set pay or grade, to rate performance, to discipline staff or to choose who to lay off.
{% endhint %}

## Three places, one release

- **This guidebook** explains EMST: how to read it, what it covers and how to use it well.
- **The EMST explorer** holds every record. It finds roles and tasks, compares two roles, switches between kinds of employer and exports what you see.
- **[GitHub](https://github.com/jrwashebek/Enviroskillstaxonomy)** is the only place EMST is changed. It holds the data files, the decisions and the issues people report.

All three are built from the same release. [About this guidebook](../release/about-this-guidebook.md)
