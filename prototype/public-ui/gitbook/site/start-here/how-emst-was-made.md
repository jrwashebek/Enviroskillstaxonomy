# How EMST was made

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](../how-to-read/drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

This page says who made EMST, how, and what has not happened yet.

## What EMST is

EMST stands for Environmental Management Skills Taxonomy. It describes environmental work. It lists the tasks people do and the skills behind them, which EMST calls competencies. It also shows how a role differs from one employer to another, such as a federal agency, a state, a nonprofit or a firm.

## Who made it

AI agents (Anthropic's Claude) researched and drafted every record and page. Each agent had a specialist role. They all worked to a charter, a set of rules, written by the EMST project lead.

Names such as “Chief Taxonomy Architect” or “EMST's federal HR agent” are AI agent roles, not people.

## Checks that ran

- Automated tests check every build. They test the data's structure and the links between records. They also look for personal data, held content and claims we must not make.
- AI agents checked facts against their sources in October 2026.
- A display rule hides every rating that has not passed a reliability study. You see “Assessment pending” instead.
- A claims list stops the site from repeating facts that could not be confirmed.

## What has not happened yet

- No one who does this work has reviewed any record.
- No reliability study has tested any rating.
- No job analysis has been done for any role.
- No person has reviewed the links to O\*NET, ESCO, OPM series or state job classes.
- No person has signed off the release.

## The review path

Each record moves up these steps. In release 2.0.0-rc.1, every record is on the second step.

- Drafted by AI agents: AI agents wrote the first version from sources.
- Checked by AI agents and scripts: AI agents checked facts against sources, and automated tests checked the data.
- Reviewed by practitioners: People who do this work read it and correct it.
- Tested for agreement: Several trained people rate it on their own, to see if they agree.
- Cleared for a stated use: It passed every check needed for one use, such as training.

Dates will appear here only once they are set.
