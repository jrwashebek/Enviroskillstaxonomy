# Who decides

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](../how-to-read/drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

## Who decides now

For now, the EMST project lead decides on changes. AI agents draft and check the work, but their checks are advice. They do not decide.

## Decisions are written down

Each decision about how EMST is built is written up on GitHub, with its reasons and its trade-offs. [Decision records on GitHub](https://github.com/jrwashebek/Enviroskillstaxonomy/tree/main/docs/decisions)

## Before a release goes public

- A release must pass eight checks before anyone outside the project may rely on it. One of them is a review by a named subject-matter expert and sign-off by the EMST project lead. [Release status](../release/status.md)
- Material from the project's pilot package goes public beyond the pilot only after the EMST project lead signs it off. The lead signs only after at least one named subject-matter expert has read it.

## Content held back

Content under the project's Tribal content hold is excluded from every build until a Tribal Advisory Council approves it. These pages neither show nor describe it; the release notes give only a count of held records.

Indigenous knowledge is a distinct body of environmental expertise held and governed by Indigenous nations. It is out of scope of EMST by design.

[Held records](../how-to-read/held-records.md)

## How you can take part

Anyone can report a problem or suggest missing work on GitHub Issues. [Corrections and contributing](../contribute/corrections-and-contributing.md)

The [governance documents on GitHub](https://github.com/jrwashebek/Enviroskillstaxonomy/tree/main/docs/governance) set out the longer-term plan for who decides, including the draft contributing guide.
