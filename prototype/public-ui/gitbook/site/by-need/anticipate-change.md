# Anticipate change

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](../how-to-read/drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

EMST keeps watch on signs that environmental work may change: new technology, new rules, funding and the job market. It does not yet say that any role is changing.

{% hint style="info" %}
Exploratory content. Everything on this page is for foresight and curriculum exploration. None of it is a basis for decisions about jobs or people. Each watch item names what would show it is not forming, and lapses if no new evidence appears by its review date.
{% endhint %}

## What EMST watches

- **Signals.** 107 signals, all on watch. Each one names its source and gives that source a grade, from A (strong) to D (weak). [Source grades](../how-to-read/source-grades.md)
- **Laws and rules.** 87 laws, rules, court decisions and other documents that shape the work, with their dated events. This release leaves out 13 more until a check confirms their facts.
- **Emerging roles.** 6 ideas about roles that may be forming, such as an AI-assisted environmental review lead. They have no tasks or competencies, because the work is not yet shown to exist.
- **Skills that may be forming.** 26 competencies are marked “On watch”.
- **Scenarios.** A set of scenarios built on two questions: public stewardship capacity, and AI reliability and legitimacy in environmental decisions. AI agents drafted them in a desk exercise. They are not forecasts.

## What EMST can't say yet

- **No role or skill has a change indicator.** EMST says a role or a skill is changing only when the evidence meets its rule. This release checked 210 possible changes to roles and skills, and none met the rule. [How change is tracked](../how-emst-works/how-change-is-tracked.md)
- **Exposure to AI and automation is pending.** No group of practitioners has rated it yet. [Assessment pending](../how-to-read/assessment-pending.md)
- **EMST makes no forecast of its own.** Signals that report other bodies' projections say so.

## Steps

1. Open What's changing in the explorer.
2. Narrow the signals by kind of evidence or by role.
3. For each signal, read its source grade and its date.
4. For each watch item, read what would show it is not forming, and its review date.
5. Use what you find to start a conversation, not to make a decision.

## Tell us what you see

If you see the work changing where you are, tell us on GitHub. Reports from practitioners are one of the kinds of signal EMST watches. [Corrections and contributing](../contribute/corrections-and-contributing.md)
