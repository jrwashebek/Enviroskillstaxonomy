# Describe the work

{% hint style="info" %}
Draft release 2.0.0-rc.1. Drafted by AI agents, not yet reviewed by people. [What this means](../how-to-read/drafted-by-ai-agents.md)
{% endhint %}

EMST can help you describe what a kind of environmental job involves. You might need this for a workforce plan, a grant report, a team charter or the first draft of a position description.

## What you can use

- **Role profiles.** 31 of the 37 roles may be used to describe work and plan. The other 6 are emerging roles, for exploring ideas only.
- **Tasks.** All 1,317 tasks may be used to describe work and plan.
- **Employer versions.** Each role says how the work differs for each kind of employer that has it.

## Steps

1. **Find the role.** Search the explorer for a job title, a task or a series code such as 0460, or browse the roles.
2. **Choose your kind of employer.** On the role's page, pick the kind of employer you work for. The notes change to match.
3. **Read the tasks and their labels.** Each task says what it may be used for and how established the work is. Values such as importance and share of time say “[Assessment pending](../how-to-read/assessment-pending.md)”. The order of the list says nothing about importance.
4. **Take the tasks with you.** Use “Copy tasks as text” or “Download CSV” on the role's page. Both keep the release, the date and the caveats with the tasks. Keep them when you share the list.
5. **Give any draft to your HR office.** Any position description built from it is a draft that must be reviewed, revised and adopted by the responsible official, and is subject to classification review.

## You can't

EMST is not ready for decisions about people. Don't use it to:

- Screen, score or rank people who apply for a job.
- Set a job's series, grade or pay (classification).
- Rate how well an employee does their job.
- Choose which jobs or people to cut in a reduction in force.

## Why

AI agents drafted these records, and no practitioner has checked them yet. That is enough to describe work in broad terms. It is not enough for decisions about people.
