## Who decides now

For now, the EMST project lead decides on changes. AI agents draft and check the work, but their checks are advice. They do not decide.

## Decisions are written down

Each decision about how EMST is built is written up on GitHub, with its reasons and its trade-offs. [Decision records on GitHub]({{decisionsUrl}})

## Before a release goes public

- A release must pass eight checks before anyone outside the project may rely on it. One of them is a review by a named subject-matter expert and sign-off by the EMST project lead. [Release status](page:release/status.md)
- Material from the project's pilot package goes public beyond the pilot only after the EMST project lead signs it off. The lead signs only after at least one named subject-matter expert has read it.
