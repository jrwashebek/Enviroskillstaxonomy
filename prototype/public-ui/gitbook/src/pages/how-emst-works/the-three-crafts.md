EMST says that environmental work draws on three kinds of skill. It calls them crafts.

| Craft | What it means |
|---|---|
| Field craft | {{craftField}} |
| Data craft | {{craftData}} |
| Statecraft | {{craftState}} |

Most jobs use all three, in different amounts. That mix is what EMST sets out to record.

## How EMST records the crafts

Each task gets a rating from 0 to 2 for each craft. The mix for a competency or a role is worked out from its tasks. Nobody sets it by hand. A planned study with practitioners will test this scale, and it may change.

## Why the crafts matter

Some skills are learned only by doing the work with someone who knows it. Field craft is the clearest case. EMST marks the tasks that need this as [formation tasks](page:how-to-read/formation-tasks.md). They show where training needs supervised practice, not just a course.

The crafts also help EMST describe work done alongside AI tools. Data craft, for example, includes checking work done by AI.
