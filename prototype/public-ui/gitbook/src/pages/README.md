This guidebook explains EMST and points you to the right records. The records themselves are in the [EMST explorer](explorer:/).

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Never for decisions about people.** {{notForPeopleNotice}}
{% endhint %}

## Start with what you need to do

- **[Describe the work](page:by-need/describe-the-work.md).** Duties for a workforce plan, a team charter or the first draft of a position description.
- **[Develop people and programs](page:by-need/develop-people-and-programs.md).** What a course or training program could cover. Explore only, for now.
- **[Find my next move](page:by-need/find-my-next-move.md).** Roles that share work with yours, and what differs. Never a recommendation.
- **[Anticipate change](page:by-need/anticipate-change.md).** Early signals about technology, policy and the job market, and what EMST can't say yet.
- **[Use, check and improve the data](page:by-need/use-check-and-improve-the-data.md).** Download, cite, reuse with AI tools, and report problems.

## What's in release {{release}}

| Records | Count | What they may be used for | More detail |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Roles | {{roles}} | {{rolesDescribe}} may be used to describe work and plan. The {{rolesEmerging}} emerging roles are explore only. | {{rolesFull}} full profiles, {{rolesLower}} earlier drafts and {{rolesEmerging}} emerging roles |
| Competencies | {{comps}} | Explore only | {{compsCore}} core and {{compsModule}} in subject modules |
| Tasks | {{tasks}} | Describe and plan | Listed on each role's page in the explorer |
| Subject modules with roles | {{modulesWithRoles}} of {{modules}} | Not a record | [What EMST covers](page:start-here/what-emst-covers/README.md) |

The counts come from the release files. AI agents drafted every record, and no person has reviewed them yet. [What the use labels mean](page:how-to-read/uses.md)

## Look things up
