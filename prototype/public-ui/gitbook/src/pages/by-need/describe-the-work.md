EMST can help you describe what a kind of environmental job involves. You might need this for a workforce plan, a grant report, a team charter or the first draft of a position description.

## What you can use

- **Role profiles.** {{rolesDescribe}} of the {{roles}} roles may be used to describe work and plan. The other {{rolesEmerging}} are emerging roles, for exploring ideas only.
- **Tasks.** All {{tasks}} tasks may be used to describe work and plan.
- **Employer versions.** Each role says how the work differs for each kind of employer that has it.

## Steps

1. **Find the role.** Search the explorer for a job title, a task or a series code such as 0460, or [browse the roles](explorer:/roles).
2. **Choose your kind of employer.** On the role's page, pick the kind of employer you work for. The notes change to match.
3. **Read the tasks and their labels.** Each task says what it may be used for and how established the work is. Values such as importance and share of time say “[Assessment pending](page:how-to-read/assessment-pending.md)”. The order of the list says nothing about importance.
4. **Take the tasks with you.** Use “Copy tasks as text” or “Download CSV” on the role's page. Both keep the release, the date and the caveats with the tasks. Keep them when you share the list.
5. **Give any draft to your HR office.** {{hrDraftRule}}
