EMST lists the competencies behind each role's tasks, with draft levels for each one. Course designers, trainers and supervisors can use it to explore what a program could cover. In this release, it is not ready for training plans.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Explore only, for now.** All {{comps}} competencies and their levels are explore only. {{developNotReached}}
{% endhint %}

## What you can do now

- **Map a course or training program to the tasks a role does.** Tasks may be used to describe and plan, so this use is open.
- **Explore what a new course might cover,** such as a class on prescribed fire.
- **Spot new skills to keep an eye on.** {{compsWatch}} competencies are marked “On watch” in [What's changing](explorer:/changing).
- **Look for formation tasks.** A [formation task](page:how-to-read/formation-tasks.md) is learned through supervised practice on the job, not from a course alone. These tasks show where a program needs field time and mentoring as well as classes.

## Steps

1. **Choose a role or a competency.** [Browse the competencies](explorer:/competencies) by subject module, or start from a role.
2. **Read the competency's draft levels.** They are marked explore only, because no expert panel has placed them yet.
3. **Check the tasks the competency supports.** They show what the skill is used for at work.
4. **Match what you teach to the tasks, not to the levels.** Tasks may be used to describe and plan. Levels may only be explored.
