    Principles

    EMST is a field notebook for a whole sector, written in pencil. The interface has one job beyond being clear: to show, without fuss, how sure we are about each thing on the page.

    • Pencil before ink. Anything drafted by AI agents and not yet reviewed wears a dotted outline and a pen mark. Reviewed work, when it exists, will be drawn in solid line.
    • Unsurveyed ground is hatched. A value that has not passed a reliability study is never shown as a number, a blank or a zero. It is a hatched area with an hourglass and the words "Assessment pending".
    • Closed doors are dashed. Uses that this release does not support, such as selection, have a dashed outline and a lock.
    • Shape and words carry meaning; colour only helps. Every status reads correctly in greyscale, in print and in Windows high contrast.
    • A ruled margin. Wide pages keep a margin column for navigation and record facts, split from the reading column by a single green line.

      Assessment pending

      Importance, time share, critical, needed at entry, craft, work function and exposure are held until a reliability study passes. In a sentence or a table cell the pending mark sits where the value would be: Assessment pending. Where a chart would be, the whole area is hatched.

      Proficiency levels

      CMP-QSAV0HJ, target for Field resource technician

      Ground-truth remote-sensed, modelled and AI-generated outputs in the field

      Defined at L1 to L4. Indicators are AI-drafted and placed at levels only provisionally.

      1. L1

        Level 1: Supervised

        Works under direct or close direction; work is checked.

        • Records the attribute at each pre-selected validation point with the reference protocol, with predicted values withheld until the field record is complete.
        • Photographs site conditions at each validation point in the required directions, so that a second observer's QA re-check can confirm the call.
      2. L2

        Target level for Field resource technician, journey level

        Level 2: Independent

        Completes standard assignments without step-by-step direction; reviewed for results.

        • Codes the likely cause of each product-field mismatch, such as timing, mixed pixels, hidden understory or location error, with an assessor confirming a sample by revisit.
        • Reports coded mismatches to the analyst with photos and locations in the agreed format, so that each can be traced to a product correction or a documented exception.
      3. L3

        Level 3: Senior

        Plans own approach to non-routine work; review is for policy fit, not method.

        • Designs a field validation sample with an analyst, setting strata, sample size and access limits so that agreement can be reported with stated uncertainty.
        • Decides where calibration plots are still needed for a remote-sensed inventory, naming the stand or site conditions where remote estimates fail.
        • Explains to product users the field conditions under which a remote or AI product is unreliable, citing coded mismatches from validation work.
      4. L4

        Level 4: Lead / Expert

        Sets the technical approach for a programme or team; work is accepted as technically authoritative.

        • Defines field reference standards for a product line, documenting acceptance criteria that state the evidence required before management or regulatory use.
        • Decides from field validation evidence whether a product is fit for a stated use, documenting the decision, its limits and the evidence a reviewer can audit.
      5. L5

        Level 5: Strategic

        Not defined for this competency.

      Notices

      Not for selection, classification or performance decisions

      EMST describes work. It is not a selection instrument, not a classification basis, not a job analysis, and not for reduction-in-force, adverse action or performance-rating decisions.

      Drafted by AI agents

      Every record and rating in this release was drafted by AI agents or carried over from a single analyst's v1 work. No person has reviewed them yet. See the review path.

      Crosswalks are candidates

      Links to O*NET, ESCO, OPM series and state class specifications were proposed by AI agents. None has been reviewed, so EMST makes no claim of alignment with those frameworks.

      This role is on watch

      The work may be forming but is not yet shown to exist across the sector. It will be reviewed by October 2027 and dropped if no new evidence appears.

      Records held for Tribal consent

      Indigenous knowledge is a distinct body of environmental expertise held and governed by Indigenous nations. It is out of scope of EMST by design.

      163 records related to Tribal employers are held until a Tribal Advisory Council approves them. These pages neither show nor describe them.
