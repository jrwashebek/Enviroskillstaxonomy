    Shared words for environmental work

    EMST describes environmental jobs: the tasks people do, the skills behind them, and how the work differs from one employer to another. It is a free, open draft.

    What you can use EMST for

    Use it for
    Describing work, planning a workforce, and mapping courses or training to the tasks a role does.
    Don't use it for
    Decisions about people: hiring, setting grade or pay, rating performance, or choosing who to lay off.
