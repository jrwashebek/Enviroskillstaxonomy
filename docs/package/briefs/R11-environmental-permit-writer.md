## Who does this work

Permit writers review applications for completeness and derive limits and conditions from technical standards and the
record. They draft enforceable permits and fact sheets, negotiate conditions, answer comments and administer issued
permits, often within a delegated program. Most of this regulatory work happens in states.

## What stays with people, and why

This describes where the work sits today. A permit condition binds a regulated party and must survive challenge, so the
draft keeps the **derivation of limits** and the **traceability of each condition to its legal or technical basis** with
the writer. That includes the calculation basis, the test against verification and challenge scenarios, negotiating
within the limits of one's authority, answering an overseeing agency's objection with a written record, and recusing
where a former employer or client has an interest. Drafting tools may change how fast a first draft appears, not who
answers for it. The role draws mainly on statecraft and data craft.

## Entry paths and formation

People learn permit writing by drafting real permits under a senior writer. The formation provisions in EMST's draft
pathways keep **two permits' conditions and fact sheets drafted unaided**, each condition traceable to its basis and
accepted by a senior writer, before reviewing AI-drafted permits is credited. A seeded-error AI-drafted permit follows,
and every condition lacking a basis must be corrected. Where live drafting is scarce, retired permits serve as practice
cases [J]. If first drafts arrive from software, a writer who never drafted from the record may not see what is missing.

## Pathways in and out (exploratory, not recommended)

- **In, compliance inspector → this role (upskill):** five steps, covering completeness and limits, enforceable
  conditions with the by-hand quota, reviewing AI-drafted permits, comments and guiding applicants, and independence.
  EMST's curriculum draft estimates about 180 hours over 12 months, mostly supervised permit work [J]. It mirrors a
  state pathway from water-quality specialist to permit writer that is registered in EMST's role set but not yet
  drafted. The role profile also links entry from monitoring and data technician.
- **Out, upskill → natural resource and environmental program manager:** same steps as the review-specialist route,
  with recognition of prior learning [J].
- **Across the boundary:** regulator-to-facility-compliance moves are linked as mirror roles. Pay is one friction: in BLS
  data for May 2025, the median environmental scientist earned $116,110 in the federal government, $80,500 in state
  government and $79,320 in consulting. The comparison is compositional, because federal staff are older and more
  senior on average [5]. EMST records costs like these in a friction register.

None of these has been tested against observed career moves.

## What you can do now

- **Supervisor.** Require that every condition in an AI- or software-drafted permit is traced to its basis before the
  draft goes to review, and record which text was machine-drafted. Keep unaided drafting in each new writer's first
  permits.
- **Training officer.** Use retired permits as practice cases. Add seeded-error drafts to training. Teach how to track
  which pending actions follow old and new procedure versions.
- **HR specialist (state).** The explorer has no State Class View for this role in this release: the Texas classes above
  are broader or partial fits, which the class views leave out, so the class pages list the role only among unreviewed
  agent candidates (not an alignment). Compare your permit-writer class specification with the draft task list and
  targets instead. Pennsylvania's Office of Administration, for example, has identified federal job titles whose experience
  may count as equivalent for certain Commonwealth titles, and reports 1,350 hires with federal work experience since
  Executive Order 2025-01 (March 2025): one model for crediting federal permit experience [6]. **Federal:** the explorer has
  no Federal Series Profile for this role either; its 0819 and 0028 rows are broader-series matches. The 0819 profile is
  built from the environmental engineer role, not this one. Take duty language from the role's draft task list in the
  explorer, subject to classification review.
- **Educator.** Use retired permits as practice cases, and require two permits' conditions and fact sheets drafted
  unaided, each condition traced to its basis, before students review an AI-drafted permit.
