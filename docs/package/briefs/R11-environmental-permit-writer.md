## Who does this work

Permit writers review applications for completeness and derive limits and conditions from technical standards and the record. They draft enforceable permits and fact sheets, negotiate conditions, answer comments and administer issued permits, often within a delegated programme. Most of this regulatory work happens in states.

## What stays human, and why

This describes where the work sits today. A permit condition binds a regulated party and must survive challenge, so the draft keeps the **derivation of limits** and the **traceability of each condition to its legal or technical basis** with the writer. That includes the calculation basis, the test against verification and challenge scenarios, negotiating within the limits of one's authority, answering an overseeing agency's objection with a written record, and recusing where a former employer or client has an interest. Drafting tools change how fast a first draft appears, not who answers for it.

## Entry paths and formation

People learn permit writing by drafting real permits under a senior writer. The WP-07 provisions keep **two permits' conditions and fact sheets drafted unaided**, each condition traceable to its basis and accepted by a senior writer, before reviewing AI-drafted permits is credited. A seeded-error AI-drafted permit follows, and every condition lacking a basis must be corrected. Where live drafting is scarce, retired permits serve as practice cases [J]. If first drafts arrive from software, a writer who never drafted from the record may not see what is missing.

## Pathways in and out (exploratory, not recommended)

- **In, compliance inspector (R12) → this role (upskill):** five steps, covering completeness and limits, enforceable conditions with the by-hand quota, reviewing AI-drafted permits, comments and guiding applicants, and independence. About 180 hours over 12 months, mostly supervised permit work [J]. It mirrors a state pathway from water-quality specialist to permit writer that is pre-registered in the role set. The role profile also links entry from monitoring and data technician (R03).
- **Out, upskill → resource programme manager (R13):** same steps as the review-specialist route, with recognition of prior learning [J].
- **Across the boundary:** regulator-to-facility-compliance moves (R11 ↔ R37) are linked as mirror roles. Pay often moves in the other direction from the one people expect, and a friction register records such costs (ADR-0023 §4).

None of these has been tested against observed moves (ADR-0023 §5).

## What you can do now

- **Supervisor.** Require that every condition in an AI- or software-drafted permit is traced to its basis before the draft goes to review, and record which text was machine-drafted. Keep unaided drafting in each new writer's first permits.
- **Training officer.** Use retired permits as practice cases. Add seeded-error drafts to training. Teach how to track which pending actions follow old and new procedure versions.
- **HR specialist (state).** Compare your permit-writer class specification with the R11 State Class View. Pennsylvania counts federal experience as equivalent and reports 1,350 hires with federal experience by August 2026, which offers a model for crediting federal permit experience [SL-031; SL-080]. Federal: use the 0819 and 0028 Federal Series Profiles, subject to classification review.
