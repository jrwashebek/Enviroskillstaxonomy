## Who does this work

Environmental review specialists decide the level of review a proposed action needs, prepare or direct effects analyses, coordinate cooperating agencies and consultations, build the administrative record and write decision documents under the governing agency's procedures. In the draft's summary, AI drafting compresses production, while verification, record integrity and litigation resilience grow.

## What stays human, and why

This describes where the work sits today. Drafting is compressible. Accountability for the record is not. The draft keeps with people the significance determination, the reasoned explanation linking evidence to each choice, the independent evaluation of sponsor- or contractor-prepared documents, confirming that every substantive comment has a reasoned response, and anticipating the claims a decision will face. Each is a judgment for which an official answers in court, under deference doctrine that now favours documented agency reasoning [V2-02]. Building a defensible administrative record now includes marking which content was produced with AI assistance.

## Entry paths and formation

People learned review by drafting whole documents under a senior reviewer. AI drafting threatens exactly that apprenticeship. The WP-07 provisions keep **two effects sections drafted unaided** from the project record, accepted by a senior reviewer without substantive revision, before reviewing AI-drafted sections is credited. They also keep **one comment set of at least 200 comments coded by hand**, with every substantive comment recovered, before machine coding is credited. Shadow-drafting on live reviews and a moot defence before counsel stand in for the formation once supplied by whole-document drafting [J].

## Pathways in and out (exploratory, not recommended)

- **In, restoration project lead (R09) → this role (reskill; the field-to-statecraft bridge):** about 160 hours over 9 months plus one live review [J]. The role profile also links entry from compliance inspector (R12).
- **Out, upskill → resource programme manager (R13):** public budgets, writing a formation plan for one entry role, knowledge continuity before departures, partner delivery, durable choices. About 120 hours over 18 months with a rotation or acting assignment [J].
- **Out (watch only):** an AI-assisted review lead role (R31) is shown only in the explorer's "What's coming" view, and its creation is a contingent move with a trigger (`scenarios.md` §8.4 CM-1).

None of these has been tested against observed moves (ADR-0023 §5).

## What you can do now

- **Supervisor.** Require that every AI-drafted section is checked against the project record and labelled in the record. Keep unaided drafting in each new reviewer's first assignments. Sample machine-grouped comments for unique points before responses are drafted.
- **Training officer.** Rebuild review training around your agency's current procedures, not the removed CEQ regulations [NEPA-01]. Add moot defences and record-gap reviews with counsel. Keep SPEED Act content as "pending" until Congress acts [NEPA-05].
- **HR specialist.** Use the 0028 and 0401 Federal Series Profiles for duty language, labelled subject to classification review. OPM's August 27, 2026 memo treats AI drafting of position descriptions as generally not high-impact when the official independently reviews and adopts the draft [V1-13; FED-05]. Selection uses still need job analysis [SEL-02].
