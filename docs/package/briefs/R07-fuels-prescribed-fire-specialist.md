## Who does this work

Fuels and prescribed fire specialists assess fuels, design and supervise fuels treatments, write and execute burn plans, and make go/no-go decisions. They also plan smoke management with air regulators, evaluate fire effects and treatment effectiveness, and work with landowners and partners across ownerships.

## What stays human, and why

This describes where the work sits today. The decisive tasks are coded *on-site required* and tied to internal qualifications. They include the go/no-go decision from a test fire, holding checks before ignition, halting an aerial ignition when the firing pattern departs from plan, and recommending a stop when a smoke forecast fails against monitors and observers. The agent-drafted notes keep **prescription judgment** and **receptor judgment** with the specialist even where drafting and forecasting improve. Accountability for a prescribed fire sits with a qualified person on the ground.

## Entry paths and formation

People learn burn planning and go/no-go judgment as trainees on qualifying prescribed fires under a qualified burn boss. Burn windows control the calendar, which is why the exploratory pathway runs 24 months [J]. Tabletop go/no-go exercises and burn-plan reviews can cover burn-window shortfalls, but they "do not replace observed burns". The formation risk is fewer qualifying burns and fewer qualified evaluators at the same time. Template-drafted burn plans add a second risk: a trainee who never writes a prescription from first principles. FS's stress test suggested treating prescribed fire as a competency to protect (`mvp_role_set.csv` rationale, [J]).

## Pathways in and out (exploratory, not recommended)

- **In, wildland fire operations (R06) → this role (upskill):** six steps, covering burn plans with two fuels maps verified in the field, smoke management and public messaging, go/no-go as a trainee on qualifying burns, monitoring treatment effectiveness, funding and delivering projects with partners, and evaluating burn crew trainees. About 240 hours over 24 months [J]. Prior learning credited: wildland fire position qualifications; a state prescribed burn manager certification where one exists.
- **In (role link, no pathway authored yet):** from restoration project lead (R09).
- **Out:** a pathway to county wildfire planning is pre-registered in the role set but not yet authored.

None of these has been tested against observed moves (ADR-0023 §5).

## What you can do now

- **Supervisor or burn boss.** When a burn plan section is template- or AI-drafted, have the trainee rewrite the prescription parameters and contingency triggers from the unit's own fuels and objectives, and record the differences. Make field verification of fuels maps part of every unit's preparation.
- **Training officer.** Count qualifying burns per trainee per season and plan tabletop go/no-go exercises to cover shortfalls, without crediting them as burns. Add smoke-forecast-versus-monitor comparisons to after-action reviews.
- **HR specialist.** No series fits closely, so draft duties from the 0401 and 0456 Federal Series Profiles, subject to classification review. Do not present qualification or licensure gates from EMST as fact until they are checked against current standards. USWFS–USFS aligned qualification standards take effect in January 2027 [V3-13; WF-10].
