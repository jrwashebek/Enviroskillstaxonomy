## Who does this work

This is a role EMST expects to be **forming**, not one that is established. Ground-truth and validation stewards design
and look after the field reference data and validation samples that remote, modeled and AI-generated products are tested
against. They code the field causes of mismatch, calibrate observers, and state whether a product is fit for a stated
use. Today the work is usually spread across technicians, analysts and program scientists rather than held by one post.

**Counter-signal recorded with the role:** validation could be absorbed into vendor services, leaving agencies with no
field calibration of their own. If that happens, the role may not form inside public employers. EMST will review the
role by 23 October 2027, and it lapses without new evidence.

## What stays with people, and why

This describes where the work sits today. The role exists because an automated product cannot validate itself. Someone
has to stand at an independent point, observe without seeing the prediction, and explain the disagreement in field
terms. Most of the tasks need identification and measurement skill formed in the field. The draft names "reject
automated detections that are implausible for the place, season or habitat, stating the ecological reason" as work that
rests on knowing the place. The fitness-for-use decision is a judgment about a decision, not a statistic. The role
joins field craft and data craft.

## Entry paths and formation

No one enters this role directly. People arrive from field technician, monitoring and data technician or geospatial
analyst work, carrying formed identification, measurement or mapping judgment. That makes the role a **downstream test
of the entry pipeline**: if seasonal and entry field openings stay scarce [1, 2], fewer people will have the field
grounding it needs. The pathway's senior step adds the formation of others: evaluating observers and capturing
site-specific knowledge from departing staff before it leaves. EMST's foresight analysis (a desk exercise by an AI agent,
not yet reviewed by a human panel) judges that treating ground truth as infrastructure (reference plots, labeled
datasets, calibrated assessor pools) holds up in all four of its scenarios, because every AI, Earth-observation and
digital measurement system depends on it [J].

## Pathways in and out (exploratory, not recommended)

- **In, field resource technician → this role (upskill):** five steps, covering reference-data design and collection with
  an analyst (one observed labeled-sample campaign), observer calibration and sampling design (one blind re-measurement
  of a crew sample), fitness-for-use decisions, formation of others and knowledge continuity, and briefing the
  accountable official on what the validation supports ("no model without a decision owner"). EMST's curriculum draft
  estimates about 200 hours over 12 months, mostly field time and mentoring [J]. Seasonal reference-data campaigns
  shared across agencies might supply field exposure where single programs lack plots [S].
- **In (role links, no pathway drafted yet):** from monitoring and data technician and from geospatial analyst.
- **Out:** none recorded; the role is too new.

None of these has been tested against observed career moves.

## What you can do now

- **Supervisor or program scientist.** Name who owns validation for each remote or AI product your program relies on,
  and whether that person ever goes to the field. Keep a reference set that the product vendor has not seen. Ask for the
  field-coded causes of mismatch, not only an accuracy number.
- **Training officer.** Use the technician-to-steward sequence to grow the function from inside a technician cohort,
  without creating a new post first. Include blind re-measurement and observer calibration sessions.
- **HR specialist.** Do not expect a series or class. Describe the duties in existing technician, biologist or data
  science positions, labeled subject to classification review. If you contract validation out, write data rights and
  independent field checks into the contract; EMST's foresight analysis lists strengthening informed-buyer capacity
  among its robust moves [J].
- **Educator.** Teach validation as field method: blind reference observation, observer calibration against
  tolerances, and coding the field cause of each map–ground mismatch.
