**North star (charter, verbatim; the only wording used externally for now):**
> "If those blends are visible and portable, mobility follows."

**Operating version (internal):**
> **If** field craft, data craft and statecraft blends are made visible **inside the instruments that gate movement** — position descriptions, class specifications, qualification standards, crediting plans, postings, assessments, development plans and learning records —
> **and if** the main frictions on specific, **demand-backed** pathways are reduced or made navigable,
> **then** movement between federal, state, local, Tribal, NGO, private and academic employers will increase on those pathways, and knowledge will be retained when work changes hands;
> **so that** environmental work is protected where it is co-delivered or devolved across actors.

**Why the narrower promise** (evidence, not preference): publishing a framework rarely changes hiring — only 37% of firms announcing skills-based hiring followed through, fewer than 1 in 700 hires changed, and Colorado rewrote about 80% of classifications while non-degree hires stayed near 25% [E: CM-011, CM-012, CM-014, CM-050]. Embedding skills in hiring operations sometimes does (Pennsylvania de-degreed ~92% of positions and ~60% of new hires lack a degree [E: CM-013]). In 2025–26 the binding constraints are demand and lost on-ramps, not visibility.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What EMST does** | describes work as tasks, competencies and role variants with crafts and frictions; puts EMST IDs into partner gate artifacts (0400 Kit, Job-Analysis Starter Pack, state crosswalks, pilot JDs and IDPs); records frictions; shows how roles are changing; keeps formation tasks visible |
| **What must be true** (assumptions to test) | crafts can be rated reliably (α gate); embedding changes applicant and hire mix (A3′); frictions on target pathways are movable (A5); destinations have openings (A6); movers perform and stay (A7); entry pathways keep producing field craft (A9); the tool is not used against workers (A11) |
| **What it does not promise** | that visibility alone moves people; that pathways are "recommended" before validation (M-03 AUC ≥ 0.70 and beating naive baselines by ≥ 0.05); any selection use before job analysis; any Tribal content before a TAC approves |
| **How we will know** | **G1** (Mar 2028; NGO pilot → federal and state): "does this help me?" ≥ 70% overall and ≥ 60% in every stratum (panel veto); EMST IDs in a real gate artifact at ≥ 50% of pilot organizations; ≥ 2 federal and ≥ 3 state signed agreements; zero misuse. **G2** (→ whole sector): a credible contribution story for higher cross-actor hire rates on pre-registered pathways; movers at parity on time-to-productivity and retention; no widening of equity gaps; EMST IDs in live artifacts at ≥ 2 federal agencies and ≥ 5 states; ≥ 5 years of stewardship funding. Full definitions: `gate_metrics_G1_G2.md`. |
| **Pre-registered failure condition** | if after 12 months no targeted pathway shows a documented artifact chain **and** the "does this help me?" pass rate is below threshold, the pathway and mobility features stop at the pilot; EMST continues as a role-change and training resource |
| **Rival explanations we must rule out** | federal contraction and series consolidation (proposed); hiring freezes and their lifting; grant terminations to corps and NGOs; federal hiring-process reform; AI compression of entry tasks; labour-market slack; fire-season demand spikes; housing and duty-station costs (logic model X1–X8) |
