## Parties

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is made on `[date]` between **`[EMST signatory institution or "the EMST project, acting through its project lead"]`** ("EMST") <!-- PENDING Q3 --> and **`[Organization legal name]`**, `[address]` ("the Pilot Organization").

## Article 1. Purpose

1.1 The Pilot Organization will use the Environmental Management Skills Taxonomy (EMST) release `[2.0.0-rc.1 or later]`, its views and kits, from `[March 2027]` to `[February 2028]`, to do real work in its organization and to tell EMST whether and how it helped, so that EMST can decide whether the tool is ready for federal and state adoption (gate G1).

1.2 This MOU is **not** a procurement, an endorsement of EMST by the Pilot Organization, or an endorsement of the Pilot Organization by EMST.

## Article 2. What the Pilot Organization will do

2.1 Name a **pilot lead** (`[role title]`) with about 0.1 FTE for ten months.

2.2 Carry out at least two use cases from the pilot design, at least one of which produces a real gate artifact: `[UC2 posting or JD | UC3 member portfolio or IDP | UC4 training plan]` and `[second use case]`, with the target artifact `[name]` by `[date]`.

2.3 Take part in the kickoff, monthly cohort calls, the midpoint workshop and close-out, and invite staff to optional research sessions (consent is individual; Article 5).

2.4 Share **de-identified, organization-level artifacts** listed in Schedule A, and the aggregate baseline figures in Schedule A, when they exist.

2.5 Report any use of EMST outputs to rank, select or remove staff, by anyone, through the misuse channel.

## Article 3. What EMST will do

3.1 Provide the release, data files, kits and views free of charge, with office hours and a named coordinator (`[role]`).

3.2 Pay a stipend of `[$ amount]` in `[instalments, dates]` for staff time; the stipend does not depend on the Pilot Organization's opinions of EMST.

3.3 Treat the Pilot Organization's role requests and feedback as change requests under `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and show their status.

3.4 Share the pilot's aggregate findings with the Pilot Organization before publication and give it ten business days to review any passage that describes it.

3.5 Enter this MOU in the EMST commitment register and describe the Pilot Organization as a pilot organization **only** after both signatures are recorded (charter Art. 12).

## Article 4. Use limits (standard terms)

4.1 **Development use only.** EMST content is used to describe and develop roles and people. It is **not a selection instrument, not a classification basis, and not for reductions in force, adverse action or performance ratings** (ADR-0012 I-2). Nothing in EMST v2.0 is selection-grade (ADR-0024).

4.2 Any AI-assisted draft produced with EMST (for example a JD skeleton) is a draft that a person at the Pilot Organization reviews, revises and adopts.

4.3 Pathway views are **exploratory**; no pathway is a recommendation.

## Article 5. Data (standard terms; see Schedule B)

5.1 **No personal data to EMST.** The Pilot Organization will not send EMST any record about an identifiable worker, volunteer, corps member, applicant or rater. EMST will not ask for one and will delete anything received in error within five business days and notify the Pilot Organization.

5.2 **Local-first tools.** Gap checks and portfolios store ratings only in files on the user's own device or in the Pilot Organization's systems.

5.3 **Research with people** (usability sessions, diary studies, interviews) is optional for each individual, runs under IRB review with informed consent, and is conducted by `[research partner]`, which holds any person-level research records under its IRB protocol. EMST receives only aggregates, with cells below five suppressed.

5.4 **Tribal exclusions.** The Pilot Organization will not share, and EMST will not keep, Indigenous knowledge content, locations or descriptions of cultural, sacred, burial or archaeological sites, consultation records, material shared in confidence by a Tribe, or documents of Tribal employers. Such material received in error is returned or destroyed (ADR-0007 V2, V4).

## Article 6. Ownership and licence

6.1 The Pilot Organization keeps ownership of its artifacts. Member-owned portfolios belong to the member.

6.2 The Pilot Organization grants EMST a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use the de-identified artifacts in Schedule A to validate and improve EMST and to publish aggregate findings and short quotations after the review in Article 3.4. Artifacts themselves are not republished without separate written permission.

6.3 Feedback that becomes EMST content (for example a new task statement) is contributed under the EMST licensing set (CC0 for data; CC BY 4.0 for publications) and the Developer Certificate of Origin.

## Article 7. Withdrawal, term and changes

7.1 Either party may withdraw at any time by written notice. Withdrawn artifacts are deleted from EMST working sets within 30 days; aggregates already published remain; unpaid stipend instalments for work already done are paid.

7.2 Term: from signature to `[31 March 2028]`, extendable in writing.

7.3 Retention: de-identified artifacts kept for three years after the term, then deleted unless the Pilot Organization agrees otherwise.

7.4 Changes to this MOU are in writing and signed by both parties.

## Article 8. Conflicts of interest

The Pilot Organization confirms it does not sell workforce taxonomy, skills-assessment or skills-inference products that compete with EMST, and will disclose any change (charter Art. 9.6).

## Schedule B — Data-use terms

| Element | Term |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Validate and improve EMST; evaluate gate G1; publish aggregate findings |
| **Data categories** | de-identified organization-level artifacts (Schedule A); aggregate counts (cells ≥ 5); usability and survey results **only as aggregates from the research partner** |
| **Permitted uses** | analysis to validate EMST content and views; aggregate reporting; short de-identified quotations after review |
| **Prohibited uses** | ranking, selecting, screening or removing any person; re-identification attempts; training third-party AI models on the artifacts; sale or transfer to third parties; any use of Tribal-related or confidential material |
| **De-identification standard** | remove names, identifiers, contact details, dates of birth, employee IDs and free text about individuals; generalize locations below county level; suppress counts below 5 |
| **Consent** | individual informed consent for any research participation, under the research partner's IRB protocol |
| **Security** | storage with access control and encryption at rest; no third-party AI processing unless the Pilot Organization opts in in writing (`[ ] opt in`); access limited to named roles |
| **Breach notice** | within 72 hours of discovery, to the Pilot Organization's contact; incident recorded |
| **Publication review** | ten business days for any passage describing the Pilot Organization |
| **Withdrawal** | Article 7.1 |
| **Retention** | Article 7.3 |
| **Tribal carve-out** | Article 5.4; nothing Tribal-related is used, under any circumstance, without the approval of a Tribal Advisory Council constituted by Tribal governments (none exists) |
| **Governing law** | `[state]` |
| **Federal or state shadow users** | not covered by this MOU; agency artifacts are covered by a separate design-partner agreement |
