## 3. Detail

**SEL-01 (OFCCP).** OFCCP published three final rules on 21 August 2026. The rule rescinding the EO 11246 regulations
(Parts 60-1, 60-2, 60-3, 60-4, 60-20, 60-30, 60-40, 60-50 and 60-999) is effective 26 October 2026; the Section 503 and
VEVRAA changes took effect 21 September 2026 [LM-180]. A private compliance site gives the same date and mentions no
delay, stay or litigation [LM-181]. A web search for a delay, stay or lawsuit found none. The register's "do not say"
still applies: the rescission removes Part 60-3 as codified for EO 11246; it does not repeal UGESP itself.

**AIGOV-03 (Colorado).** The Department of Law's rulemaking page, last updated 21 October 2026 [LM-182], lists: interim
draft rules released 6 October; a regulatory analysis released 21 October; written comments through 26 October (11:59
p.m.), extended to the last day of the hearing if it runs longer; the hearing on 26 October at 10:00 a.m. (in person and
virtual); and rules required "before January 1, 2027". HB 26-1263 (the Chatbot Safety Act) was signed on 1 July 2026 and
takes effect on 1 January 2027. The page shows no postponement. The outcome of today's hearing could not be confirmed.

**NEPA-10 and NEPA-05 (permitting bills).** NACo's tracker (updated 13 October) lists no committee action or votes on the
Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act and says sponsors expect a lame-duck vote in November [LM-183]. The Energy
Workforce & Technology Council (22 October) reports a Senate cloture vote set for 9 November, but it neither cites S. 5653
nor says who scheduled the vote [LM-184]; treat it as unverified. The SPEED Act's latest action is still its referral
to Senate EPW on 18 December 2025 [LM-185].

**FOR-01 (objection rule).** No new information. The final rule (FR Doc 2026-20890) was verified on 23 October; its
effective date, 12 November, has not arrived.

**NHPA-01 (Section 106).** Web searches returned nothing newer than the September and early October items already in the
register. The ACHP news page and the Federal Register could not be read, so "unpublished as of 9 October" stays as the
verified statement.

**DISC-01 (California).** Vinson & Elkins (19 October) confirms OAL approval and effect on 12 October, the 10 November
deadline for Scope 1 and 2 reports from U.S. entities with more than $1 billion in revenue doing business in California,
and that SB 261 remains under the Ninth Circuit's preliminary injunction. Scope 3 reporting from 2027 is left to a
separate CARB rulemaking [LM-186].

**UAS-01 (Part 108).** Two trade sites show RIN 2120-AL82 still pending at OIRA (to 18 October [LM-187]; to 13 October
[LM-188]). They note the nominal 90-day review ended 8 October and that a 30-day extension, if used, would end about 7
November. The FAA said in September that it hopes to publish by the end of 2026; that is an aspiration, not a date.

**FED-04 (OPM series).** OPM's CHCO memo listing shows no memo on occupational-series modernization or consolidation from
3 August to 22 October 2026 [LM-189]. The listing says it is not comprehensive. News searches found no final decision.

**STD-01 (2028 SOC).** The BLS revision page, modified 30 September 2026, still lists the second Federal Register notice
as "TBD" and the final structure notice for 2027 [LM-190].

**CWA-01 (WOTUS).** No final rule and no OIRA submission found. The Harvard EELP tracker was last updated 9 September
[LM-191].
