## Domain Expert Bench (DB): summary

**Evidence limit.** The team's web-search budget was used up before I started, so I ran no searches. Fetches of agency and issuer pages were refused. What I could read: BLS OEWS May 2025 [E], the v1 database and docs, and the OpenLMP plan corpus. All claims about credentials, frameworks, current rules and AI adoption are [J]. The 46 sources behind them (DB-020 to DB-065) are listed as unverified and form a ready queue for a verification pass.

**Subsector map.** 44 subsectors in 9 clusters:
- Water 6, Pollution control 6, Environmental health 2, Energy & extractives 5, Climate & hazards 3, Markets & corporate 2, Lands 11, Built environment 2.
- Cross-cutting practice 7. These are work functions, not subsectors.
- v1 covers 11 fully, 22 partially and 11 not at all.

**Biggest federal/state gaps.** 18 subsectors are High priority; v1 fully covers only 2 of them (wildland fire, NEPA review).
- **Not covered:** drinking water, wastewater, hazardous waste, environmental public health, emergency management/hazard mitigation/floodplain, transportation environmental.
- **Partly covered:** air (one competency), remediation, water-quality regulation, mining and abandoned mine lands, Section 106, compliance inspection, GHG accounting, adaptation.
- Even forest plans need this work: 1,377 OpenLMP plan components cite minerals or reclamation, and 373 cite air or smoke.

**Top proposals:**
- **P-DB-02:** 18 stewardable modules. Wave 1 is water utilities, water regulation, air, waste/remediation, environmental health and emergency management, plus forests/fire and fish/wildlife seeded from v1.
- **P-DB-03:** organize by orthogonal facets (work function × subsector × competency type) and retire v1 domains as the top level.
- **P-DB-04:** add *Operate & maintain* and *Respond & recover* to your work-function facet.
- **P-DB-06 / P-DB-07:** add a regulatory-position facet and credential-gate attributes.
- **P-DB-09:** move DB-C03, C24, C25 and C29 into the core, and add a core competency for drafting and administering enforceable authorizations.
- **Priority candidate competencies:** DB-C01 to C07, C12, C18 to C20, C27 to C29, C40 to C43, C50.

**Where v1 generalizations break worst:**
- S1 to S6 cannot represent operations, disclosure or incident cycles; 3.6.2 and 6.7.5 carry NEPA flags.
- Fieldcraft is defined around landscapes, so it leaves out judgment about engineered systems.
- Levels by years of tenure clash with licensure grades.
- Being competent is treated as being legally authorized, which licensed subsectors separate.
- The AI-exposure picture is skewed: only 7 of 666 tasks are robotics-exposed.
