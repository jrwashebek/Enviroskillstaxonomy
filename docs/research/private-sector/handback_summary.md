**Translation and pay**
- v1 treats "private" as F14/F15, which are statecraft-heavy. But consulting plus architecture/engineering services employ about 34% of environmental scientists, more than state government [E].
- At the median, consulting pays no premium. May 2025 medians: federal $116,110, state $80,500, consulting $79,320 [E].
- Private premiums sit in oil & gas and utilities, in senior corporate sustainability, and in sign-off or revenue accountability.
- Federal-to-consulting moves often cut pay. I found no evidence on how many displaced federal staff are actually moving [S].
- Most translations are mirror roles across the regulator–regulated line: the competency is shared and the posture is inverted. The common gaps are business practice, licensure, and the shift from regulator to applicant posture.

**AI effects on private entry-level work** [J; corroborating evidence is weak and vendor-heavy]
- Junior apprenticeship work (drafting, tables, records review, maps, ESG data collection) is what AI compresses.
- Expect fewer juniors per project manager, field technician work becoming the main on-ramp, a reviewer shortage in 5–8 years, and pressure on time-and-materials billing.
- Stamps, signatures, site visits and independence stay human.
