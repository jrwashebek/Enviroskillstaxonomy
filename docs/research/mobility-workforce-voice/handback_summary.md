## CM summary

**Theory of change.** Necessary but not sufficient, and "visible" is the weakest link.
- Visibility alone barely moves hiring: under 1 in 700 hires changed after firms announced skills-based hiring (CM-011); removing degree requirements alone added about 2 points (CM-014); Colorado's share of non-degree hires stayed at 25% after rewriting 80% of classifications (CM-012).
- What works is putting skills into the instruments that gate movement: assessments, class specs, position descriptions (PDs), applicant tracking.
- Portability is mostly a property of institutions, not skills.
- In 2025–26 the binding constraints are demand and lost on-ramps: federal staff fell 12%, the Forest Service and Park Service each lost over a quarter, under-30s are 7.9%, there were no non-fire seasonals in 2025, and corps were cut. Work is moving to counties and states.
- That mobility improves environmental outcomes is unevidenced [S].
- A restated, testable version and a contribution-analysis plan are in `logic_model.md`.

**Transferability** (v1 tags; single analyst; families are not jobs):
- **Two islands.** A field/science cluster and a statecraft/human-systems cluster, with none of the 33 above-chance links crossing between them.
- **Field craft is hardest to enter.** Other families hold 7.6% of field families' Core competencies; the reverse is 15.9%.
- **One bridge kit.** The field→statecraft gap is mostly five teachable collaboration competencies (5.2.1–5.2.5).
- **Portability and entry-level compression coincide.** The most portable competencies carry the most career-ladder-destruction flags (36% of the top 25 vs 28% overall).
- **Craft blends can't tell destinations apart** (31% of family pairs are near-identical), so similarity should use asymmetric, importance-weighted task overlap, validated against observed moves.

**Expansion gates.**
- **G1 (NGO pilot → federal/state):**
  - "does this help me?" ≥70% overall and ≥60% in every group, with a panel veto;
  - EMST used in a real PD, posting or plan at ≥50% of pilot orgs;
  - pathways validated against real moves (AUC ≥0.70), or labelled exploratory;
  - zero misuse.
- **G2 (→ whole sector):**
  - a credible case that cross-sector hires rose versus comparison units;
  - no widening of equity gaps;
  - ≥2 federal agencies and ≥5 states using EMST in live documents;
  - five years of stewardship funding.

**Top proposals.** P-CM-02 (embed in hiring instruments), 03 (validate before recommending), 04 (similarity basis), 05 (friction register), 06 (bridge kits), 07 (protect entry routes), 09 (binding panel), 12 (use the 2025–26 displacement as validation data).

**Limits.** The team web-search budget ran out after 11 searches and many fetches were blocked. 23 sources are cross-referenced from FH/FC/FS/AS. Evidence on firefighters' and diversity experience is thin.
