# CR-DB-001: Module-structure gaps found while building the wave-1 editorial sections (WP-06)

- **From:** DB (A6), WP-06 · **To:** CA (decider); DE (schema and validator); routed items to SC/LM (WP-10) and LC/IO (WP-07) · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Type:** X (structural) and C (clarification) · **SemVer impact:** minor if item 2 adds an optional field; otherwise none
- **Affects:** ADR-0003 G-4; ADR-0018 §2, §6; PHASE4_BUILD_PLAN WP-06 acceptance; `schema/src/emst.schema.src.yaml#Enactment`; `scripts/validate_v2.py`; `docs/decisions/candidate_disposition.csv` (DB-C53)
- **Evidence label:** J (DB; each item was found by authoring the wave-1 records and running the validator on them). Generated input: `docs/build/WP-06/three_module_coverage.csv`.

## Problems, interim choices and proposals

1. **The three-module rule has no CI check and no exception register.** WP-06 acceptance says "no core competency enacted in fewer than three modules without a recorded exception", but `validate_v2.py` has no such rule and no file holds exceptions.
   - *Interim (WP-06):* every core competency WP-06 enacts is enacted in three or more wave-1 modules. The coverage table is generated by `docs/build/WP-06/coverage.py`. Authored core competencies with no module enactment are listed there as "below-three-in-wave-1"; WP-06 did not author enactments where the module adds nothing to the work.
   - *Proposal:* (a) DE adds `W-THREE-MODULE` (error at `candidate`): a live core competency with at least one module Enactment must have module Enactments in three or more distinct modules, or a row in a new exception register; (b) the CA creates the register, e.g. `data/v2/governance/three_module_exceptions.yaml` (competency, rationale, decidedBy, decidedOn, reviewBy); (c) the CA rules whether a core competency with **zero** module enactments passes (DB recommends yes for cluster X and transversal practice, which ADR-0018 §2 says are enacted in every module, and that CI reports recurrence as a computed view rather than requiring enactment records everywhere).
2. **Enactment records cannot cite a subsector.** The acceptance criterion "every module record cites a subsector code" can be met for Competency and Task records (the validator checks `E-MODULE-SUBSECTOR`) but not for Enactment (no `subsectors` property; closed schema) or Indicator records.
   - *Interim:* each WP-06 enactment states its subsectors in `editorialNote`; indicators inherit them from their competency.
   - *Proposal:* add optional `subsectors` (array of `v_subsector`) to Enactment, required when `enactmentKind = module`; ratify inheritance for Indicator.
3. **DB-C53 is placed two ways.** ADR-0018 §2 says "generate legally defensible analytical data" is a **core** competency (three-module rule), while `candidate_disposition.csv` places DB-C53 in MOD-18 `lab` (wave 2). Water, environmental health, waste and air compliance sampling all depend on it.
   - *Interim:* WP-06 links field custody and QC to core FC-C24 enactments and does not author DB-C53.
   - *Proposal:* the CA rules on DB-C53's home and amends the disposition or the ADR.
4. **Cross-module support.** IA-CC-05's tasks live in `forest` (they carry the wave-1 public-lands enactment) while the competency's home is `extract` (wave 2). The validator allows module-to-module references. *Proposal:* confirm this as the pattern for early drafts named in dispositions.
5. **Scope question for the CA (not authored).** MOD-12 has no module competency for wildlife population and harvest management (season and bag setting, game population management). Role R15 relies on core v1 1.4.1, 4.1.2 and 1.1.5. Stay with the core, or add a candidate? Adding one is net growth in a module, not the core.
6. **Routed to WP-10 (SC registry owner; LM):** the L5 instrument registry lacks the regimes the wave-1 modules turn on, so module text is regime-neutral and enactment notes cannot link them: drinking water lead and copper and PFAS rules and primacy; discharge permits, water-quality certification and dredge-and-fill programmes; stationary-source permitting (new source review, operating permits) and implementation plans; hazardous waste generator and corrective-action rules; state packaging producer-responsibility laws; the national flood insurance programme and federal mitigation funding changes of 2025-26; the wilderness statute; federal grazing and mining-law regulations. Each needs a dated status (ADR-0028) before WP-14 uses it.
7. **Routed to WP-07 (credential register):** the module briefs in `docs/modules/` list the gates each module needs, with issuer and verification status from V3-08 (operator certification issued by states, exams by Water Professionals International (formerly ABC); REHS/RS by NEHA; CFM by ASFPM; CHMM by IHMM; PWS by the SWS PCP; CWB by TWS; Certified Forester by SAF; ISA Certified Arborist; LEED AP and ISSP credentials by GBCI). Only `seed:crd-hazwoper` is referenced from tasks today; other gates use `reproductionRegime.credentialGate` and `signOffAuthority` until register records exist.

## Consequences if not accepted

Items 1–2: the WP-06 acceptance criteria can only be checked by the WP-06 generated table, not by CI. Item 3: compliance-sampling roles (R24, R26) keep an unresolved dependency. Item 6: module enactments stay unlinked to the instruments that drive change in their subsectors.

## CA ruling

- **Ruling:** **accept 1, 2 and 4; decide 3 and 5; route 6 and 7** · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Decider:** CA · **Recorded in:** ADR-0030 §1.3 and §4, ADR-0018 (amendment to §2), SPEC §5.4 and §18.2 (draft.3), `data/v2/governance/three_module_exceptions.yaml` (created), DECISION_LOG `CR-DB-001`
- **Implementation:** DE per `RULINGS_FOR_DE.md` §15; DB (A6) content in S2 as listed.

1. **Three-module rule in CI: accepted, with the zero-enactment case decided.**
   - `W-THREE-MODULE` on drafts and `E-THREE-MODULE` from `candidate` on: a live core competency with `competencyType = practice` whose live module Enactments name only one or two distinct `enactedInModule` values, with no row in the exception register.
   - **Zero module enactments passes.** Enactments are written only where the module changes the performance (ADR-0007 §B.4), so having none means the work is the same everywhere, not that it is module-specific. Transversal practice and knowledge areas are exempt: the rule applies to their task cores (ADR-0025).
   - **Recurrence is reported, not stored.** The validator reports `I-THREE-MODULE-RECURRENCE` per core practice competency: the distinct modules among its module enactments, the home modules of the tasks that support it and, once WP-08 lands, the modules of role profiles that target it. Release notes count core practice competencies with computed recurrence below three, for the Editorial Board. It is not a release gate at rc.1, because only wave-1 modules exist.
   - **Register:** the CA has created `data/v2/governance/three_module_exceptions.yaml` (`exceptions: []`). Each row: `competency`, `modules[]`, `rationale`, `decidedBy` (`pid:` or `CA`), `decidedOn`, `reviewBy`, `decidedIn`. Only the CA (later the Editorial Board) adds rows. On 2026-10-23 no core competency has one or two module enactments, so the register starts empty (`docs/build/WP-06/three_module_coverage.csv`).
2. **`Enactment.subsectors`: accepted.** Optional array of `v_subsector`. `E-MODULE-SUBSECTOR` extends to module Enactments: error from `candidate` on, warning on drafts. Indicators inherit their competency's subsectors as a computed value; no Indicator field. **DB (A6)** moves the values out of `editorialNote` in S2.
3. **DB-C53: home stays MOD-18 `lab` (wave 2); it is not core.** ADR-0018 §2 is amended.
   - The laboratory's task core (method execution, method validation, laboratory QA/QC, accreditation) is performed by one job family under one accreditation regime, whatever module the data serves. It fails the three-module test.
   - The part that recurs across modules is the data user's: field custody and field QC (core `seed:FC-C24`, already in place) and review of laboratory data for use in a decision. The second goes into core `seed:v1-2.2.3` (quality of environmental data from collection to release) as a task ("Review laboratory data packages, qualifiers and holding times to decide whether results can support a compliance decision") and one L2 indicator.
   - **Who:** DB (A6) writes them in S2 as part of WP-08 roles R24 and R26; DC (A4) reviews. Core size does not change.
4. **Cross-module support: confirmed** as the pattern for early drafts named in the dispositions. Module-to-module references are allowed in both directions; only core → module is forbidden (SPEC §3.2). IA-CC-05 stays homed in `extract` with its tasks and public-lands enactment in `forest`. It ships in rc.1 as a named early draft (build plan §5.4).
5. **Wildlife harvest management: add a module candidate, not a core one.** `DB-C56` "Set harvest regulations and manage game populations from survey and harvest data", MOD-12 `wildlife`, wave 1. It is net growth in a module, so G-9 is unaffected. DB (A6) authors it in S2 for R15 at P2 starter-kit depth; FC (A3) reviews the field-survey anchors. The CA adds the disposition row at WP-16 with the other amendments (`docs/decisions/integration/disposition_amendments.csv`).
6. **Routed to WP-10 (LM):** accepted as listed. Each regime needs a dated status (ADR-0028) before WP-14 cites it. Module enactments link instruments with typed roles (CR-SC-001 ruling, item 2).
7. **Routed to WP-07 credential register:** LC (A8) adds register records for the module gates in item 7 that are not yet there in S2. DB then links tasks to them through `credentialGates`.
