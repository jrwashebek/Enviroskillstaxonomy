# CR-FIX-B-002: Reconcile the NGO pilot's time commitment and state what participants receive

- **From:** Fix-B (AI agent, briefs/deck/PD editor, package fix pass) · **To:** AS (owner of the Stage 1a pilot design);
  project lead · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Type:** document text change · **SemVer impact:** none
- **Affects:** `docs/adoption/stage1a_ngo_pilot_design.md` §4 (criterion 3), `docs/adoption/pilot_mou_template.md` 2.1;
  deck slides 17–18
- **Evidence label:** J (raised by package review issues 8 and 20)

## Problem

The pilot runs from March 2027 to February 2028 (twelve months; MOU 1.1), but the selection criteria and the MOU ask for
"a named lead with about 0.1 FTE for ten months". Package review issue 20 says the mismatch will confuse NGO readers.
Issue 8 also asks the offer to state what participants receive. The design's support model (§6) lists a kickoff
workshop, monthly cohort calls, weekly office hours, research sessions, peer pairs, a midpoint workshop, a request-a-role
route and a tiered stipend; it does not mention profiles fitted to each organization's roles or returning participants'
data, which the review suggested.

The deck now states the commitment as the design gives it ("about 0.1 FTE … the design asks for ten months of it within
the pilot year") and lists only what the design provides. Fix-B cannot edit `docs/adoption/`.

## Proposed change

1. Either change "ten months" to the pilot's full term, or say which ten months (for example "from kickoff to the end of
   the last use-case cycle; the final two months are reporting only").
2. Add one "What participating organizations receive" line to §6 and to MOU Article 3, and decide whether the review's
   two suggestions (role profiles fitted to the organization; the organization's artifacts and aggregates returned to
   it) are part of the offer.

Fix-B (or the WP-14 owner) updates deck slides 17–18 to the reconciled wording.
