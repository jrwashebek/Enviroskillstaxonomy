## 1. Verdict

**rc.1 is not ready to go to design partners as a dataset. It needs one blocker fixed and the labelling of agent drafts made field-level.**

What works:
- The build is byte-reproducible.
- The ID registry is sound.
- The release is referentially closed.
- The headline counts are exact.
- The generated views keep every gated value hidden.
- The OpenLMP bootstrap intervals reproduce to the last digit.

What fails:
- **The normative bundle fails its own published schema for 981 of 10,472 records.**
- **The labelling of agent drafts is too weak.** The bundle and the HR-facing CSVs carry agent-drafted values that the display gate exists to hide, labelled only at record level. These include time shares that sum to exactly 1.0 per role, critical and needed-at-entry flags, work function, and field and formation dependency.
- **Several provenance labels overstate human involvement:**
  - v1 LLM-assisted tags are labelled `J` (expert judgment);
  - X1 is labelled `semapv:ManualMappingCuration`, but an AI agent made it;
  - three passes of one model are recorded as three raters.
- **The change-signal and lifecycle methods are sound in code but weak in their inputs:**
  - the independence key is a self-assigned slug;
  - 56 signal records repeat 16 observations;
  - every "emerging" or "proposed" role rests on watch signals that do not meet the vocabulary's own entry thresholds;
  - 198 competencies are "established", which the vocabulary defines as "Validated and stable".

The gate report's statement that rc.1 "is internally consistent" is not supported.

**Counts: 1 blocker, 11 major and 15 minor findings.**

## 4. Content-quality sample

- **Seed: 20261024.** `random.Random(20261024).sample()` drew 40 of the 224 released competency IDs, then 60 of the 1,313 task IDs, each from the sorted list. Script: `$S/c9_sample.py`. Manual judgments are listed item by item in `$S/c9_score.py`; the sampled IDs are in `$S/sample_ids.txt`.
- **Rule for the production-versus-review split.** A record has a defect when:
  - it has a task whose deliverable current AI can draft or compute (document, summary, analysis, estimate, model, map, dataset, code or report); and

  Checks embedded inside the production task do not count. Records with no such deliverable pass.
